## Original adaptation and frozen cases

The controlled study is our adaptation informed by the above cues and existing kit methods. None of these sampled sources supplies this exact sequence or establishes universal stroke widths, golden ratios, overlap rules or shape beauty. No sampled evidence was found that golden ratio should govern all logos.

- Faithful outlined-initial refinement: retain valued loop/integration/overlap; trial contour/weight separately; reject a filled replacement that loses those features. Actual counter closure remains unverified until rendered.
- Authorized structural alternative: record explicitly released features per candidate; do not silently apply those releases to the faithful track.
- Kerning-only locked mark: use pair-spacing comparisons, preserve icon paths and omit unrelated contour or ratio studies.
- Intentional overlap that merges at small size: preserve the baseline relationship, log reproduction limits, and propose an authorized variant rather than removing it without authority.

These are frozen expected decisions examined in text-only reviewer walkthroughs and local instruction inspection, not executable scenario tests or executed logo trials. Independent review must remain text-only and receive no raw book content or client evidence. Structural checks, reviewer findings and actual native artwork acceptance stay separate.
