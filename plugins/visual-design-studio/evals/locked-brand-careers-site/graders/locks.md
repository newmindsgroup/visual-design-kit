Passes only if the response:
- Keeps the locks: uses navy #12284C, orange #F26B21, white and Source Sans 3 as given, and does not introduce a new brand hue or second typeface. Tints, shades and neutrals derived from the given colors are acceptable when labeled as derived.
- Explores inside the locks rather than inventing a new identity: two or more executions or variations of the locked system (for example photography-led versus typographic, different layout rhythms, scale or proportion of orange), as the brief mandates the brand.
- Checks contrast of the locked colors and acts on it: orange #F26B21 on white is about 3.0:1, so it fails for body text and small text (fine only for large text, graphics or with navy text on orange, about 4.8:1), while navy on white passes easily.
- Gives careers-site specifics: role search and filters, employee photography or stories art direction, an application CTA, and accessibility or inclusive-imagery notes.
Fails if it proposes a new palette, a new accent color or a new display typeface.
