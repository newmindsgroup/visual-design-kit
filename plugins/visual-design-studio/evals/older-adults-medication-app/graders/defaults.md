Passes only if the response specifies: body text of at least 18px/pt (or iOS Dynamic Type support with large defaults), touch targets of at least 44pt/48dp (larger preferred), high contrast meeting at least WCAG AA 4.5:1 (ideally higher), plain-language copy, few choices per screen, and no reliance on color alone or on gestures without visible controls. Fails if it uses small text, dense layouts or decorative low-contrast styling.
