Passes only if each of the two colors is specified in at least four color systems: HEX (sRGB), RGB, CMYK given as separate coated and uncoated builds (not one conversion for both stocks), Pantone Coated (C) and Uncoated (U), and RAL or NCS for the signage. Any Pantone, CMYK, RAL or NCS value must be labeled as a nearest match to confirm against the physical guide or the printer's proof, not presented as exact.
Fails if it gives one CMYK build for both stocks, or presents Pantone or RAL matches as exact with no instruction to verify.
