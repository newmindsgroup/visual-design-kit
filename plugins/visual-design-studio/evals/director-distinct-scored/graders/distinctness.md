Passes only if the response shows the directions are distinct with an explicit pairwise comparison (a table or list covering A vs B, A vs C and B vs C) across axes such as type, palette, layout, imagery, motion and voice, and each pair differs on at least three axes.
Fails if distinctness is asserted without a pairwise comparison, or any pair differs on fewer than three axes.
