Passes only if the response:
- Presents three named directions, each with two named typefaces, 3-5 hex colors with roles, a layout idea, an imagery idea and a line of sample copy.
- Shows the directions are distinct with an explicit pairwise comparison (a table or list) across axes such as type, palette, layout, imagery, motion and voice, with each pair differing on at least three.
- Scores the directions in a weighted matrix with stated weights (summing to 100 or otherwise explicit) and criteria that include objective or audience fit, differentiation and accessibility, with a one-line reason per score or per criterion.
- Includes a sensitivity check: says whether a plausible change in a weight would flip the winner, naming the criterion (for example "B wins unless differentiation matters less than accessibility").
- Recommends one winner, names a runner-up, and says what would change the decision.
- Grounds at least one direction in the London legal or startup category (for example SRA-regulated firm conventions, Magic Circle navy-and-serif clichés to avoid, or startup founders as the audience).
