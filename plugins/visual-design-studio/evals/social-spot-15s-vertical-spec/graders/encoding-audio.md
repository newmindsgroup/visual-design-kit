Passes only if the response gives the delivery encode (H.264 MP4, about 10-20 Mbps for 1080x1920, AAC audio at 48 kHz) and a loudness target measured with a BS.1770 meter (about -14 LUFS integrated for social, true peak no higher than -1 dBTP), and keeps a high-quality master (for example ProRes 422 HQ) as the archive.
Fails if there is no loudness target or no bitrate.
