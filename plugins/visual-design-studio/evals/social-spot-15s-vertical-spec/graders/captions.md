Passes only if the response requires burned-in open captions for sound-off autoplay with concrete rules (2 lines maximum, about 20-32 characters per line, roughly 48-64 px on the 1080-wide canvas, a box or stroke for contrast) and also a sidecar caption file (SRT or WebVTT, or the platform caption editor), and keeps the first-frame headline short (about 7 words or fewer).
Fails if it relies on the voice-over alone or on auto-captions with no correction step.
