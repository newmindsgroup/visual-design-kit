Passes only if the response does both:
- States at least one contrast or legibility caveat with a value or rule (for example gold or champagne on ivory fails as text at about 2.3:1; checked contrast ratios for the proposed pairs; a minimum type size for mandatory text).
- Gives a shelf or thumbnail test (view at shelf distance, blur or grayscale, e-commerce thumbnail) or a note on regulatory panel space (ingredients, net contents in ml and fl oz).
Fails if the contrast caveat has no value or rule.
