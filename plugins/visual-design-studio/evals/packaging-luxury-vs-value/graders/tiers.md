Passes only if the response gives two clearly different directions with concrete values for each:
- Luxury: a named high-contrast or refined display face (for example Bodoni Moda, Didot, Cormorant, Canela) with restrained weights; a limited palette with hex values such as near-black, ivory and a metallic or deep accent; very generous whitespace and few elements; material and finish specifics (heavy glass, weighty lid or collar, foil or blind emboss, rigid box); understated price communication.
- Value: a named bold or condensed sans (for example Barlow Condensed, Anton, Archivo, Oswald); saturated hex values with high shelf contrast; a large readable benefit and size callout; simple low-cost structure and print (PP tub, few colors, no foils); legibility from shelf distance.
- At least one contrast or legibility caveat stated with a value or rule (for example metallic gold on ivory fails as text; small type minimums; checked contrast ratios).
- A shelf or thumbnail test (view at shelf distance, blur or grayscale, e-commerce thumbnail) or a note on regulatory panel space (ingredients, net contents in ml and fl oz).
Fails if both directions share the same type and palette with only a finish change, or if values are adjectives only.
