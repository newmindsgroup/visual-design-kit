Passes only if the luxury ($380) direction gives concrete values: a named high-contrast or refined display face (for example Bodoni Moda, Didot, Cormorant, Canela, Playfair Display) with restrained weights; a limited palette with hex values such as near-black, ivory and a metallic or deep accent; very generous whitespace and few elements; material and finish specifics (heavy glass, weighty lid or collar, foil or blind emboss, rigid box); and understated price communication.
Fails if the typeface is described only by category ("a refined serif") or the palette has no hex values.
