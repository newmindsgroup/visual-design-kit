Passes only if the response asks 2 to 4 focused qualifying questions before producing a final logo (for example: company name and what it does, audience, personality or competitors, where the logo will be used), offers concrete options or a recommendation for at least one question, and does not bury the user in a long questionnaire. Fails if it asks more than 6 questions or produces a finished logo with no questions at all.
