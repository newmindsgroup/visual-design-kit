Passes only if the response gives a named story arc that moves from the client's problem and stakes to the approach, then proof (case results with numbers), then the offer and a specific next step or ask, and lists 12 slides that follow it, each with a headline written as an assertion (a sentence that states the point) rather than a topic label.
