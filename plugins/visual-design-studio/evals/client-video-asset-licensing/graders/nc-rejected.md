Passes only if the response rejects the CC BY-NC track because a client's paid promotion is commercial use, recommends filtering Freesound to CC0 (or a cleared license), and notes that CC0 waives copyright but not trademark or patent rights, and that publicity or privacy rights (for example a recognizable voice) are still the user's to clear.
Fails if it allows the BY-NC track with attribution, or treats CC0 as clearing every right.
