Passes only if the response:
- Sets an explicit reading-level target of about grade 5-6 (ceiling around grade 8) or an equivalent plain-language standard, and short sentences (about 15 words or fewer on average).
- Structures the form as one question or one topic per screen with a visible step count and a save-and-return option, and lists the documents needed before starting.
- Writes first-screen copy that actually meets that level: short words, active voice, "you", no jargon such as "eligibility", "household income threshold" or "remit" without explanation. Judge the copy itself, not the claim.
- Pairs icons or pictures with words (never icons alone) and keeps body text at 16px or larger (18px is a bonus).
- Provides help beyond the screen: a phone number, in-person or community help, and translated versions written in plain language in each language (not machine translation of complex source text).
- Mentions at least one standard: plainlanguage.gov / Plain Writing Act, ISO 24495-1, or WCAG 3.1.5.
