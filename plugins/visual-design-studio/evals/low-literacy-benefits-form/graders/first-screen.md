Passes only if the first-screen copy the response writes actually meets a grade 5-6 level: short words, short sentences, active voice, "you", and no unexplained jargon such as "eligibility", "household income threshold", "applicant" or "remit". Judge the copy itself, not the claim about it. The response must also pair icons or pictures with words (never icons alone) and keep body text at 16px or larger (18px is a bonus).
Fails if the copy uses unexplained jargon or long sentences, or the response says nothing about icon-plus-word pairing or a minimum body size.
