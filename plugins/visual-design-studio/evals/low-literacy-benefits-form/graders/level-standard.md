Passes only if the response does both:
- Sets an explicit reading-level target of about grade 5-6 (ceiling around grade 8) or an equivalent plain-language measure, and short sentences (about 15 words or fewer on average).
- Names at least one plain-language or accessibility standard: plainlanguage.gov or the Plain Writing Act of 2010, ISO 24495-1, or WCAG 3.1.5 (Reading Level).
Fails if it only says "plain language" or "simple words" without a numeric level, or names no standard.
