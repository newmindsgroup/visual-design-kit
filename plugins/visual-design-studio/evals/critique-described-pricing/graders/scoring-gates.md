Passes only if the response scores or rates the page against named criteria (for example brief fit, distinctiveness, hierarchy, typography, color and contrast, accessibility, layout, craft) AND explains at least one gate or cap that limits a score regardless of how the page looks, tied to this page. Accepted examples: the WCAG AA contrast failure caps the accessibility score (for example at 2 of 5); four or more template tells cap distinctiveness (for example at 2); a score is held provisional because nothing was rendered.
Fails if it gives scores with no stated gate or cap, or gives no criterion-level scores at all.
