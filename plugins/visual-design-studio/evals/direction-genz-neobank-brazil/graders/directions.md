Passes only if the response:
- Presents at least 3 named directions that are clearly different from each other (type, palette, layout, imagery or voice differ), not one idea in several colors.
- Grounds them in the brief's specifics: Brazil (Portuguese copy, Pix or local payment culture, Brazilian market context) and Gen Z students.
- Gives concrete design values for each direction (named typefaces, hex or OKLCH colors, or similar), not adjectives only.
- Scores or compares the directions against explicit criteria and recommends one with a reason and a runner-up or trade-off.
- Does not default to a purple/indigo gradient, glassmorphism or a generic "centered hero + three cards" look without a stated reason.
