Passes only if the response does both: says a frame 1/16 in (about 1.6 mm) inside the trim sits in the danger zone and will look uneven after cutting, so it moves to the safe area (at least 0.125 in inside trim) or is removed; and says a 12 mm QR code is too small, raising it to at least 20 x 20 mm (or justifying a smaller size by a short scanning distance and short URL) with a 4-module quiet zone, dark on light.
