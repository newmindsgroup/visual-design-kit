Passes only if the response says the 5 pt light reversed text fails and raises it to at least 8 pt in a medium or heavier weight (10 pt or more on uncoated stock, no fine serifs), and says the 0.1 pt hairline is below the printable minimum of 0.25 pt for positive lines (0.5 pt reversed).
Fails if it leaves the 5 pt reversed type or the 0.1 pt line as they are.
