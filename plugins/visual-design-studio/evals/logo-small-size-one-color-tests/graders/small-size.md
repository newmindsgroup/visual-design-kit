Passes only if the response tests the symbol at 16x16 and 32x32 px (favicon) and 24-48 px (UI, avatar circle crop), says fine detail merges below about 24 px or 8 mm, and calls for a simplified small-size master (open counters, heavier hairlines, fewer details) or a separate monogram for small uses. It gives minimum sizes in both px and mm, for example symbol 16 px favicon and 24 px elsewhere, 6-8 mm in print; wordmark 80-100 px wide, 20-25 mm.
Fails if it approves the detailed badge at favicon size unchanged, or gives no minimum sizes.
