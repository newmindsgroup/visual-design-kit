Passes only if the response covers embroidery limits with numbers (lines under about 1 mm and text under about 5 mm cap height usually fail; confirm with the embroiderer) and therefore a simplified secondary mark or the symbol alone for polos, plus a small print test (8-10 mm on uncoated stock) checking fill-in and ink traps or the thin serif wordmark.
Fails if it has no numeric embroidery limit.
