Passes only if the response does both:
- Cites Quebec's Charter of the French Language as amended by Bill 96 (Loi 96): French must be available and at least as prominent as (markedly predominant over, for some uses) any other language on commercial websites, product information, contracts and ads aimed at Quebec, and hedges with "verify" or a pointer to the OQLF or counsel.
- Mentions Quebec's Law 25 (privacy, cookie or consent banners, tracking off by default) or a language selection that does not hide French behind English.
Fails if Bill 96 / the Charter is not named.
