Passes only if the response:
- Cites Quebec's Charter of the French Language as amended by Bill 96 (Loi 96): French must be available and at least as prominent as (markedly predominant over, for some uses) any other language on commercial websites, product information, contracts and ads aimed at Quebec, and hedges with "verify" or a pointer to the OQLF or counsel.
- Requires native Canadian French (fr-CA) writing or transcreation, not France French and not raw machine translation, with at least one concrete fr-CA example (for example courriel, magasiner, fin de semaine, or vous in commerce).
- Gives French typographic rules that change the layout: longer strings (about 15-30% expansion), a non-breaking space before : ; ! ? and inside « guillemets » (or the OQLF's narrower rule), accents kept on capitals.
- Covers at least two commerce specifics: Interac payment, GST plus QST tax display, CAD pricing, or French-language product images and packaging.
- Mentions Quebec's Law 25 (privacy, cookie or consent banners) or language selection that does not hide French behind English.
