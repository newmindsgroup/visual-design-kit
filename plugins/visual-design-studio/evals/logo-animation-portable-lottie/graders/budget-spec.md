Passes only if the spec gives a file-size budget measured as transferred bytes (for example 10-30 KB for a micro-interaction, 30-100 KB for a spot animation; a loader at the low end), the frame rate (30 or 60 fps), the duration and loop or one-shot segments, and colors mapped to brand tokens or a dotLottie theme for dark mode.
Fails if it has no file-size budget.
