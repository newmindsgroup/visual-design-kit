## Scope and sequence

Content on supplied screens only. Do not design fixtures, structures, shelving or installation engineering. Fixture photographs supply context only. Keep client sources private and reusable instructions client-agnostic.

Intake and approved brand evidence precede direction. Prepare assets and compose the actual format, then storyboard and animate when needed. Adapt and localize accepted work, review the rendered result, export and verify on the actual player. Touchscreen projects branch after discovery into task flows, states, media, handoffs and device validation. Precise edits preserve accepted upstream work and rerun only affected checks.

## Operating contract

Every selected stage receives the objective, screen specification, approved baseline, change scope, source provenance, required output and acceptance criteria. Every handoff returns editable files, previews, exact versions, assumptions, failed checks and next owner. Missing source footage may hold final editing while concept work proceeds. Missing hardware holds player acceptance, not all design work.

Public market material may inform research. Reuse requires appropriate rights; otherwise create original supporting work and obtain accurate authorized product assets. No downloading private albums into the reusable package. No skill grants publication, purchases or third-party access.

## Validation status

Authored project-local instructions. Not installed into the frozen release package and not demonstrated production capability. Structural checks and regression results are recorded in checks (source-repository evidence, not bundled or inherited). Video timing was not validated from the reference previews. Actual touchscreen and player acceptance require runtime evidence.

## Portable candidate boundary

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T23:23:36-04:00

Historical descriptions above describe source-library authoring only. On a receiving computer, begin with tool and execution status unverified. Run the selected scenario and retain its actual artifacts before claiming local verification. Structural source checks do not establish provider, device or user acceptance. The candidate manifest identifies exact source and adapter hashes; earlier results do not transfer to changed bytes.
