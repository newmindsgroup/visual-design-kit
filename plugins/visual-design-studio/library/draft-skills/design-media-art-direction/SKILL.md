# Generation-ready art direction

## Inputs and workflow

Read the [media workflow](../../MEDIA-QUALITY-WORKFLOW.md) and reuse accepted upstream inputs. Use the [production packet](../../templates/media-quality-packet.md) for concrete inputs and outputs. Missing evidence stays explicit; tool availability is not production acceptance.

Translate the approved direction into a composition plan before selecting a generator. Specify audience, message, output dimensions, medium, viewing assumptions and the image's role. Use display-discovery and display-art-direction only when the requested output is for a physical screen; generic website, identity and infographic assets do not require a display brief. Distinguish product truth from visual mood. Draw or describe a thumbnail with focal subject, horizon, foreground/background, negative-space region and copy exclusion zones in normalized coordinates. Reserve typography as editable composition unless the task explicitly calls for image-integrated lettering.

Describe light direction, source size, contrast, material response, palette roles and intended visual tension. Replace vague words such as premium with observable decisions. Produce contrasting directions when direction is unresolved; do not spend on cosmetic variations of the same idea. State what must remain exact, what may vary and what should be absent. Use the shared packet to compile only model-supported controls. Return composition plan, asset brief and candidate acceptance criteria; unresolved brand rules remain provisional.

## Handoff and acceptance

Return artifact locations, exact baseline, candidate status, observed checks, remaining defects and next owner through the existing [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Apply the [rubric](../../templates/media-quality-rubric.md). Preserve original assets and follow the [media execution contract](../../MEDIA-GENERATION.md). Stop at the agreed revision/budget limit and report an unresolved result rather than silently changing the brief.


## Photographic production requirement

For product and environment imagery, use convincing photographic realism unless the brief explicitly approves another medium. A vector outline, CSS shape or schematic product is a layout prototype, never a substitute for photographic production. Keep vector logos, editable text, diagrams and interface controls in their appropriate medium.

Before generation, distinguish an actual product from a fictional concept. For actual products, obtain approved photos or sufficiently precise identity references and preserve geometry, packaging, label facts and materials. Missing identity evidence holds product-truth acceptance; a plausible generated object is not verification. Specify perspective, light direction and size, material response, contact shadows, surface, scale and copy space. Do not prescribe a universal dark palette, luxury treatment or dramatic light across brands.

Produce one hero still per selected direction and inspect it before deriving motion or format variants. Technical playback, export fidelity and native editability cannot advance a visually rejected baseline. A visibly credible still is a candidate, not automatic human approval.

Apply the scope, evidence and decision-combination rules in the [shared rubric](../../templates/media-quality-rubric.md).
