# Retail and physical display communication

Separate three modes at intake: passive signage, shared interactive kiosk and personal-device continuation. Record site/layout, viewing approach and occlusion, estimated then measured distance, mounting height, ambient light/glare, dwell distribution, noise, available input, privacy and accessible alternative. Label estimates as hypotheses. A screen-size preview cannot validate viewing distance or accessible reach.

For passive content define one primary message, legible qualification, loop duration and entry at any frame. Every frame that carries a claim needs its relevant qualification. Do not defer essential meaning to a later frame a passerby may never see. Storyboard time windows before animation; do not adopt a universal word count or seconds-per-slide rule. Test comprehension with the actual audience and exposure context when available.

For kiosks map approach, attract, start, task, confirmation and reset; distinguish deliberate activity from idle. Document touch target geometry at physical scale, seated/standing access, timeout warning/recovery, privacy after abandonment, offline failure and assisted alternatives. A CSS target size is not a physical accessibility measurement. For mobile continuation preserve context and explain destination; QR is not the sole accessible path.

Attach a context worksheet to the existing research record: condition, supplied/observed/inferred status, impact, provisional design response, measurement needed, owner and blocked acceptance. Reuse the adaptive display artifact; add a storyboard and field-test plan. Output preparation can complete internally, but installed-display approval requires actual hardware/site evidence. No fabricated interviews or measurements.
