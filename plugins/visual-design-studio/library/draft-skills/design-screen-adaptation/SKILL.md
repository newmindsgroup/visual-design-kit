# Campaign adaptation

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce campaign adaptation within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `display-art-direction` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Separate campaign invariants from flexible composition. Audit rights before reusing market assets. If assets are unavailable, derive an original direction from researched principles rather than copying distinctive execution. Map product, headline, brand and CTA to new zones; rebuild rather than stretch.

## Output

Adaptation rationale, invariant list and editable format-specific layouts. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Compare brand fidelity, message priority and cropping across formats; identify inferred brand rules. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.

## Media quality extension

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T21:08:20-04:00

Use a [campaign reference pack](../design-media-reference-control/SKILL.md) to lock product truth, lighting, palette roles and material treatment while adapting layout. Review the whole campaign as a contact sheet. Variation should serve the format or message; inconsistent treatment from changing generators is a defect, not creative variety.
