## Method

Freeze criteria and exact artifact versions before testing: recognition, differentiation hypothesis, legibility, optical balance, brand fit and reproduction. Inspect the actual artwork apart from mockups. Compare same-content alternatives; do not label taste an objective scalar or memory/recognition proven without audience evidence.

Select realistic smallest/largest uses, monochrome and reversed versions, light/dark/complex backgrounds, raster exports and actual display or print output where needed. Check fine details, counters, unintended shapes, kerning and loss after rasterization. Use relevant fabrication constraints for embroidery, engraving or signage, but supplier samples are required for those acceptance claims. Logo-specific accessibility exceptions do not excuse inaccessible surrounding text or interactive controls.

Record method, expected criterion, observed result and evidence per variant. Separate rendering, native source, supplier/device, audience and clearance status. Similarity searches are limited screening, not legal clearance. Classify fixes by severity, preserve originals, and rerun only affected checks after correction. Missing physical evidence leaves that use unverified, not the whole concept unusable.

## Comparative logo acceptance

Read [reference synthesis and proportion studies](../design-logo-refinement/references/reference-and-proportion.md) and use the [logo study record](../../templates/logo-study.md). Confirm reference coverage and retained meaning before judging fit. Evaluate icon, exact full name, horizontal and stacked composition where relevant; compare optical spacing and junctions, not just uniform gaps. Check whether repeated decorative symbols weaken hierarchy.

For each proposed variant, inspect clean artwork at its intended minimum size and at one smaller stress size, recording actual pixel or physical dimensions and viewing conditions. Test one-color and reversed versions for counters, botanical recognition when required, stroke loss, merges and name legibility. Compare equivalent realistic applications at the same scale, separating mockup persuasion from artwork quality. Measured golden-ratio studies require baseline/construction/optical comparisons; evaluate the result rather than rewarding ratio compliance. Native reopen, screen rendering, physical fabrication and audience acceptance are separate evidence classes, with missing checks left open.

## Initial-reading criterion

When the brief requires a recognizable initial, freeze the exact intended letter as an acceptance criterion before monogram exploration. Evaluate the standalone symbol without its adjacent name or explanatory narrative at intended sizes; record the actual observed reading and uncertainty, and seek user or suitable-reader confirmation separately. Decorative abstraction that obscures the initial fails that criterion even if its geometry or mockup is attractive. Do not claim broad audience recognition from an agent's reading. Pair shortlisted symbols with the full approved name using the [typography specimen method](../design-typography/references/type-craft.md#brand-name-and-symbol-pairing-specimens); typography selection cannot conceal a failed symbol criterion.
