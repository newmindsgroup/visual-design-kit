# Similarity screening and reproduction

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:32:14-04:00

Screen against the actual category, geographic markets and intended uses. Record sources, queries, date and inspected marks. Compare silhouette, construction, lettering and overall impression rather than only pixel matching. Ask suitable readers about unintended forms, cultural meanings and pronunciation where needed. A negative search does not prove uniqueness, availability or legal clearance; retain screening limits and route clearance separately.

For embroidery, engraving, cut vinyl, illuminated signs and other selected processes obtain supplier constraints: minimum features/gaps, material, color layers, tool tolerances and viewing conditions. Prepare only authorized simplifications; retain an unaltered master. Request samples or proofs for relevant uses and record measured results. Do not substitute generic minimum line widths or a monitor mockup for supplier acceptance.

Deliver a use-by-variant matrix showing screen-render, supplier-proof and audience review separately. Photograph or scan physical evidence with stated capture limits, not claims of perfect color equivalence.
