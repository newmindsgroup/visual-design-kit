# Reference synthesis, continuity and proportion studies

Version-Timestamp: 2026-10-02 17:56:47 AST

Use for existing-logo refinement or new directions when references, retained meaning or geometric construction affect the decision. This method supplements the identity brief; it grants no redesign or external-action authority.

## Reference coverage and meaning

Visually inspect every user-provided reference before synthesis. Keep a coverage table with opaque ID, viewed artifact/page/frame, date, viewed/partial/inaccessible status, observed cue and intended use. Text descriptions, thumbnails and filenames are not full visual coverage. If a reference cannot be opened, record the gap and narrow the synthesis or hold a decision that depends on it. Keep private images, names and source locators in the project workspace.

Separate cues that support the actual business and audience from cues admired for another context. For an established service business, assess authority through coherent lettering, confident silhouette, stable composition and reproduction, grounded in its brief. A retail, app or social reference may offer a useful curve or spacing idea without supporting its whole aesthetic. Do not equate modernity with removing meaningful features.

## Continuity and complete composition

Within authorized revision scope, terms such as modernize, refresh or update start on the faithful track; ask only when change authority is materially ambiguous. Use the original as the preserved baseline and record the disliked draft separately. Criticism of a draft does not reject the original identity. Preserve an original-faithful improvement track alongside materially different alternatives when exploration is authorized, unless the user explicitly rejects the existing identity. If redesign is outside scope, keep alternatives as proposals awaiting authority. Record retained, released and unresolved equity (such as name, initial, botanical cue and color), rights status, permitted changes and rejected candidate IDs separately. Rejection does not establish rights to reuse prior artwork.

When botanical meaning is valued, preserve a recognizable botanical cue while testing simplified contour, junction and counterform treatments. Strengthening an outline, lowering a loop or removing leaf detail alone may not resolve weak composition. Diagnose the interaction of icon geometry, typography, visual weight and spacing before proposing edits.

For an initial-based identity, explore the standalone initial and horizontal and stacked lockups with the complete approved business name when relevant. Do not repeat the decorative symbol beneath itself merely to fill a stacked composition. Compare hierarchy and recognition with and without repetition. Adjust pair spacing, icon-to-name separation, line spacing and junctions by apparent negative space. Equal numeric gaps can look unequal; retain measured and optically corrected variants for comparison.

## Golden-ratio construction, when requested

Apply the study to both faithful refinement and new directions. For every option presented as a measured study, measure its overlay before presentation. Exploratory sketches may defer measurement only if explicitly labeled unmeasured and provisional. Define the relationship before drawing: for example, icon-to-wordmark height, two arc radii or a lockup bounding rectangle. Choose relationships that support the idea; do not force every gap, letter or curve into phi.

Keep an inspectable vector overlay in a separate named layer and an export alongside the clean artwork. Label anchors, units, dimension definitions and actual measured values. Record ratio A/B, target approximately 1.618, deviation and whether the relationship was a construction constraint, approximation or rejected experiment. A hypothetical 100-unit height and 161.8-unit width illustrate a construction target only; they are not measurements of delivered artwork.

Compare the baseline, constructed study and optically corrected candidate at the same scale. Log overshoot, junction, weight or spacing corrections with actual revised measurements, including departures from phi. Never infer unmeasured ratios from appearance, fabricate post-hoc dimensions, or claim golden-ratio construction guarantees beauty, modernity or professionalism. If measurements or editable overlays cannot be produced, mark the study pending rather than complete.

## Tool and evidence contract

Honor a requested native tool when available and authorized. Illustrator on Mac is one example, not a universal requirement. If the requested native tool is unavailable, disclose this before artwork production and mark native save/reopen pending. Proceed with another tool only when the existing authorization permits fallback; otherwise hold dependent artwork and request a tooling decision. Record requested versus actually used app/version, master format, native save/reopen result and fallback status. A generated SVG, screenshot or mockup does not prove Illustrator execution, native editability or fabrication.

Use the [logo study record](../../../templates/logo-study.md) to carry comparisons, coverage and measurements into evaluation. Preserve originals and date/attribute outputs in the project workspace.

## Signature structure before contour edits

Before changing contours, identify the valued morphology (recognizable forms) and topology (connections, loops, counters and intentional overlaps) in the preserved original. Record each feature as retained, explicitly released or unresolved, with evidence of the user's intent. Under a faithful or contour-only request, identified signature features default to retained unless explicitly released; unresolved applies to materially ambiguous intent, never silence. A loop, leaf-to-letter integration or overlap may carry recognition; do not assume every overlap is a defect or solid fill is inherently stronger. Compare an overlap with the preserved original and available applications: introduced or worsened collisions differ from retained connections. Small-size merging of an original overlap is a reproduction observation, not permission to remove it. Keep signature structure in the faithful track. Retaining a leaf and wordmark alone does not justify calling a candidate original-faithful if its distinctive construction has changed.

Start with moderate contour or stroke adjustments that preserve counters, negative space and the relationships between elements. Closing a counter, filling a meaningful gap, changing a leaf-to-letter connection or repositioning a signature loop so its relationship changes is structural, regardless of the numeric adjustment. Compare apparent mass and leaf clearance at actual small size; heavier geometry can crowd the botanical cue or merge into a blob. Record the before/after weight and gap observations rather than treating added mass as improvement. Keep matched-scale original/edited closeups and complete lockup comparisons so viewers can inspect the actual changes.

Obtain explicit user authorization before changing signature structure. If a feature's value is unresolved, propose the structural change separately while retaining it in the faithful candidate. Explicit authorization for a different direction does not remove the faithful track's retained locks. Golden-ratio construction cannot override valued morphology or topology; reject or optically adjust a construction that compromises them.

## From viewed evidence to a renderable idea

Use [the evidence, concept and critique loop](../../design-logo-concepts/references/evidence-concept-critique.md) for concrete curve/proportion/spacing/shape annotations, attributed preference and exclusion records, attachment proof and fixed typography. Visual coverage alone cannot prove a renderer received the image. Keep the original-faithful protections above for their authorized track; an explicitly rejected/released source must not be imposed on a separately authorized restart. Record a meaningful concept or scoped drawing hypothesis before each render, then inspect actual output before selection presentation.
