## Method

Work from the selected concept or supplied approved master, exact text and change authority. Preserve a baseline copy. Distinguish a fidelity repair from a redesign. Do not trace or reconstruct a supplied mark without authorization.

Inspect silhouette, negative space, apparent weight, curve continuity, tangent behavior, corners, joins and unnecessary points. Use grids to explain geometry only when useful; do not retrofit arbitrary golden-ratio circles. Compare optical alignment with mathematical alignment at intended sizes. Adjust kerning and custom lettering deliberately, including diacritics and language forms; changes to font software and artwork have different rights implications.

Use an appropriate authorized vector tool. Inspect paths, compound paths, clipping, transforms, stroke behavior and embedded/raster elements. Preserve an editable working master and intentional outlined delivery variant where required. Check spelling before outlining and preserve exact live-text source where appropriate. Record each geometry change and rationale. AI-generated pixels are concept material until a genuine vector master is produced and inspected.

## Output and acceptance

Versioned vector candidate, refinement comparison, geometry/text invariants and unresolved small-size or reproduction issues. Native reopen and render checks remain explicit execution requirements.

Carry file versions, approval scope and unresolved checks through [stage handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md).
