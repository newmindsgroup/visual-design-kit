## 1. Read evidence and pin the decision

Reuse the known business brief. Ask only for missing inputs that would change the next drawing: annotated visual likes/dislikes, actual logo references beyond collateral mood, intended minimum sizes and reproduction constraints. Do not repeat answered questions or block independent authorized sketches on an optional use specification. Required references, rights, locked typography or change authority hold the dependent work until resolved.

Record reference viewing separately from renderer attachment. Inspect the actual image, log its version/hash and the region inspected. Annotate at least one specific mechanism per used reference: width relative to height, counter aperture, arc transition, terminal angle, stroke contrast, overlap topology, interletter negative space or icon-to-name gap. Describe what the mechanism does for recognition, hierarchy or reproduction. Measured values need units and image/native measurement evidence; estimates must say estimated. Add what to learn, what to avoid copying and audience relevance. Mood adjectives alone are insufficient. References provide evidence, never tracing rights or guaranteed originality.

For each rendering call that relies on a reference, log the actual attached file/hash, provider input slot/attachment ID or native import/layer ID and tool request/UI receipt. Verify the receipt corresponds to the pinned image. A filename in prose, URL in a prompt, viewed image or provider response does not prove attachment. Preserve failed/missing attachments and hold reference-dependent rendering. A supported authorized verbal-only experiment can proceed as a separately labeled branch, without claiming reference fidelity. Do not retry an uncertain submission.

Keep append-only preferences with who said what, source/time, exact shown revision/hash, preference versus requirement, scope and active/released/conflicting/superseded state. Translate exclusions into visible structural vetoes, such as an enclosed counter, squared terminal or disconnected join. An author's inference is a hypothesis, not user direction. Resolve material conflicts with the user. Rejected drafts never become preferred sources through renaming. Releasing a rejected treatment permits only the explicitly authorized exploration; it does not unlock unrelated equity.

## Local book evidence when selected

Prefer relevant existing local identity/logo/type books over a new bulk intake. Verify catalog identity and available source/extraction, retrieve only decision-relevant sections and inspect illustrated pages when the principle depends on visible examples. Record title/author/edition or unknown, actual page/section locator, text versus visual coverage, source hash, original adapted principle and the specific concept/critique decision it changes in the study. A catalog pointer alone is not reading. Do not send pages, full extracts or private locators to image/review providers; send only approved original bounded method summaries. Book examples inform a procedure and do not authorize copying a logo. Do not claim the whole library was used from sampled pages.

## Sampled book principles

These are original procedural adaptations from bounded selected sections, not new canon cards or a claim that the entire library was read. Source copies, hashes and local locators stay in the private work packet.

- Kevin Budelmann and Yang Kim, *Brand Identity Essentials, Revised and Expanded*, printed page 130 in the consulted edition: specify drawing and spacing relationships, then compare the clean result and document optical corrections. A construction grid alone cannot pass visual acceptance; do not copy another logo's ratios.
- Alina Wheeler, *Designing Brand Identity*, fifth edition (2018), printed pages 150 and 151: inspect full-name letterforms, whitespace, legibility and system use. Adaptation: give wordmark-only equal standing and test the full name at matched sizes. An attractive isolated symbol cannot substitute for that check.
- David Airey, *Identity Designed: the Process* (2024; edition unverified), selected Strategy and Design case discussions (printed labels unavailable; source-specific locators retained privately): consider positive and negative shape together. Adaptation: test the silhouette and counter reading in a small single-color view; preserve distinctive structure within authority. A case diagram proves neither universal recognition nor reproduction.

Title/edition support was separately checked in source front/copyright matter. The applied methods are our adaptation and the artwork examples remain local. No source pages or extracts belong in a provider prompt.

## Bounded supporting research

Use genuine identity case studies and close artwork details to form an annotated mechanism, not a gallery of collateral moods. Log public source/date, text versus visual coverage, source claim, inferred exercise and limits in the study. The following primary pages were read as text on 2026-10-05; their complete image sets were not visually inspected in this method task. These are supporting research, not attached renderer references, purchasing recommendations or proof of any client result.

| Source | Supported observation and bounded exercise |
| --- | --- |
| [Pentagram, Garden Museum](https://www.pentagram.com/work/garden-museum) | Its published rationale describes avoiding literal floral themes and using landscape-inspired forms for growth/organic life. Inference: when the brief supports nature, test abstract growth through an original contour or counter relationship rather than automatically attaching a leaf. Do not copy the case's shapes or import its museum audience assumptions. |
| [Monotype, Logotype Services](https://www.monotype.com/products/logotype-services) | Describes refining lines/curves, letter-shape consistency, whitespace and visual relationships. Exercise: compare intended shared curve/terminal/stress logic at matched visible height and inspect pair-specific space; pin any approved differences. These checks support craft, not a quality guarantee. |
| [Wilcom, Lettering](https://wilcom.com/explore/lettering) and [Standard Fonts](https://docs.wilcom.com/embroiderystudio/27/en/OnlineHelp/Management/sample_fonts/sample_fonts-1.htm) | Describe small-letter stitch spacing and push/pull adjustments, plus font families for different reproduction methods. Exercise: ask the supplier to check counters, joins, spacing, stitch compensation, material and actual size. Do not infer a universal minimum or accept a digital preview as a stitched sample. |

For a final finalist, a qualified identity designer or type specialist can provide focused critique of the actual letterforms and lockups within existing data/authority. This is a recommendation for a later decision, not hiring or expense authorization.

## Scope branch: symbol or whole name

Wordmark-only, monogram, symbol and combination directions are equally valid when the brief supports them. Never require an icon alongside a successful whole-name logotype. Record explicit whole-name typography exploration authority separately from existing source locks. That authority releases only its named scope, not the exact name, history or other equity. Pin the actual font or custom-lettering native construction/specimen per exploratory candidate before its comparison render; its approval remains pending. Existing locked lettering continues to use its fixed source. An exploratory drawing is not a faithful source reproduction.

## 2. Author an idea before each render

Write a concise concept card with a recorded timestamp before a provider call or local/native render. Bind card ID/hash to the render request receipt and record request/response times from the environment clock. A later/equal card time fails the order check; insufficient clock precision leaves order not-tested until an ordered tool/event receipt establishes it. Declare faithful or exploration track and its specific change-authority evidence. Reuse an unchanged card for a mechanical re-export with the prior receipt/hash verified; do not invent new ideas for that step. State the intended meaning, the original shape/lettering mechanism, the evidence motivating it, the visible experiment, expected effect, retained features, exclusions and one failure condition. Each distinct direction must change an idea or structure rather than just decoration or weight. For refinement, record a drawing hypothesis instead of inventing a new brand story. A construction study or golden-ratio overlay explains geometry and cannot establish visual quality.

When the reported problem is the glyph or typography, begin with letterform silhouettes, counters, joins, terminals, stress and proportion only within the unlocked scope. A kerning-only request remains kerning-only. A defect finding cannot release a lock; structural redesign needs explicit exploration authority, otherwise record the deficiency and hold that change. If the original is explicitly rejected and released, do not force its forms back into an authorized new direction. When an initial is required, compare it alone without an icon or adjacent name, then test its relationship to the complete name. For an authorized whole-name logotype, study the complete word structure and pair relationships directly; do not introduce an initial or icon by default. Add decorative integration only after a legible structure exists. Pin the exact approved font family, style, version, variable axes, casing, glyphs, spacing and source file hash or approved outline master/hash. Verify an actual specimen and record the font actually loaded by the local/native assembler or the approved outlined master actually used. Compare source/hash/parameters and exported pixels to the frozen brief; a provider echo of the requested family is not load evidence. Image providers unable to verify exact type should omit it for assembly from the pinned source. Render the name deterministically from that source where possible; image models may explore the symbol but must not regenerate locked lettering. A substitution or unverified source leaves the type-dependent comparison provisional. Authorized lettering changes remain separate from a typography lock.

## 3. Render a controlled comparison

Freeze one presentation recipe before rendering: background/color profile, canvas dimensions, artwork visible height or bounded fit rule, margins, crop policy and placement. Use the same recipe across candidates. Show the exact complete name in a shallow horizontal and stacked composition, plus the standalone mark at the intended small size in a single color. For wordmark-only work, the standalone mark is the complete wordmark. Stack only at authorized word boundaries; for an indivisible single name record stacked as not applicable with that reason, and use a compact full-name view without splitting letters or inventing extra text. That compact view is not evidence of a stacked lockup; this justified omission alone does not hold presentation unless the project specifically requires a stacked solution. No view requires an added icon. Set actual dimensions from the brief and report them; a smaller stress size is separate evidence. Never omit a word to fit, stretch lettering or silently scale one candidate to look stronger. If a lockup cannot fit the shared recipe, record that failure and adjust the recipe for all candidates or hold that view. Compare context mockups only after clean artwork.

Render only within existing provider/data/cost authority. Log concept author/session separately from actual renderer, provider/model/tool, versions, inputs, output hashes and limitations. Unknown runtime stays unknown. A polished raster mood output is concept evidence; it is not editable vector artwork or a production master. Do not upscale a weak idea in place of deciding its direction.

Compare models dynamically only when that comparison is authorized and useful. For concept-author comparisons, freeze the same brief/evidence/authority and output contract; distinct concept cards are outputs. For renderer comparisons, also pin the same authored card and prompt. Match exact source typography, attached image hashes, task, dimensions and preview recipe; log supported settings, seed if supported, attempts and repairs. A changed brief, missing attachment or unsupported setting makes the comparison unmatched. Separate concept quality from adherence and render defects, and expose limitations. A narrow trial can support a task-specific preference, never a universal best-model claim. Choosing a different provider requires its own applicable authorization.

## 4. Critique before presenting

Inspect the actual clean output before presenting it for selection. For each candidate, bind findings to output/hash and image location: intended initial reading, silhouette, proportions, curve/join quality, counters, spacing, complete name, exclusions and the three preview views. Record observed effect, pass/revise/not-tested, correction and a retained-feature veto. Self-critique must finish before selection presentation and stays distinct from required independent review. Record the project's presentation-review rule: a required review must complete before shortlist presentation unless explicit current-project/current-phase authority permits an exploratory presentation with that review pending. Disclose the exact exception and pending state; it cannot grant final completion or production readiness. Missing policy defaults to holding the shortlist for required review, while permitted drafting continues. If pixels are unavailable, record not-tested and hold image-based judgments. A process hold or progress update may disclose missing evidence without presenting a candidate as ready.

Keep weak/rejected/superseded/unapproved dispositions visible and attributable. Self-diagnosed weakness is not user rejection; supersession does not erase earlier rejection. Preserve parent/revision IDs, exact bytes and feedback. Choose a focused correction or retire the idea; keep each repair and its actual result. Present only a clearly labeled shortlist that passed this presentation gate, with limitations and a focused decision. An explicitly requested work-in-progress view still requires completed inspection and disclosed failed criteria. Do not rationalize a visible failure with a post-hoc story.

Keep [four acceptance states](../../design-logo-evaluation/references/review-contract.md#separate-acceptance-states-and-drawing-evidence) independent. Record any scoped brainstorming review exception with granting speaker/source, current project, revision, allowed phase, duration or pilot bound and gates it actually changes. Ignore another project's exception and preserve unmet production gates even when the current brainstorming exception is valid. It does not authorize a new data destination. Native vector construction/reopen, exports, rights and intended-use checks remain required downstream. Embroidery readiness needs a stitched sample for the actual size, fabric and technique. Provider completion, high resolution, self-score or a digital mockup supplies none of those approvals.

## Receipt ownership

These receipts feed the existing states; no receipt alone grants user acceptance or production readiness.

| Receipt | Existing state informed |
| --- | --- |
| Viewing/attachment, preferences, concept card, type-source match and preview recipe | Criteria evidence; missing inputs hold dependent checks |
| Self-critique and independent-review result, recorded separately | Critique completion; self-critique cannot satisfy independent review |
| Explicit user decision bound to shown revision/hash/scope | User visual acceptance only for that decision |
| Native/export/rights/use checks and required stitched sample | Production readiness, together with the required approvals |
| Brainstorming exception, history and model trial | Context for named checks; never acceptance on its own |
