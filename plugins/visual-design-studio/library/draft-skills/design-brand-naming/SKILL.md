# Naming and brand architecture

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:25:03-04:00

Status: authored project-local instruction. No installation or production acceptance implied.

## Inputs and routing

Use the [identity workflow](../../IDENTITY-WORKFLOW.md) and [identity handoff record](../../templates/identity-handoff.md). Reuse accepted inputs with version and scope; dependencies are information requirements, not mandatory reruns. Existing [identity](../design-identity-system/SKILL.md) coordinates selected stages.

## Method

Reuse accepted positioning, audience, market/language scope and existing names. Distinguish corporate brand, product, service, endorsed brand and campaign before naming. Map relationships, endorsement rules and expansion needs. A precise logo task with a fixed name does not activate renaming.

Develop distinct naming territories, then candidates with rationale, pronunciation, spelling, spoken-use, ambiguity and cultural/language questions. Screen only within authorized sources and record exact query/date/market and limitations. Domain or handle availability is not trademark clearance; no purchase or registration follows from selection. Do not claim uniqueness, translation quality or legal availability from model confidence.

Compare candidates against the same frozen criteria. Record proposed, shortlisted, approved-for-design and clearance-pending separately. Route slogans, taglines and ongoing voice to content writing. Pass exact spelling, capitalization, diacritics, permitted abbreviations and endorsement hierarchy to logo concepts. An unavailable specialist can hold final adoption while clearly labeled exploration proceeds.

## Output and acceptance

Naming decision and architecture map, candidate rationale, screening evidence/limits, exact accepted text, decision owner and clearance status.

Carry file versions, approval scope and unresolved checks through [stage handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md).
