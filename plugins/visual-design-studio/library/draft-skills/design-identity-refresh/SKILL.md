# Identity refresh and migration

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:25:03-04:00

Status: authored project-local instruction. No installation or production acceptance implied.

## Inputs and routing

Use the [identity workflow](../../IDENTITY-WORKFLOW.md) and [identity handoff record](../../templates/identity-handoff.md). Reuse accepted inputs with version and scope; dependencies are information requirements, not mandatory reruns. Existing [identity](../design-identity-system/SKILL.md) coordinates selected stages.

## Method

Inventory current masters, touchpoints, official guidelines, partial/conflicting rules and known recognition evidence. Separate stakeholder preference from audience evidence. Identify retain/refine/replace proposals and scope authority before drawing.

Compare continuity and deliberate change through equivalent applications. Explain what improves and what recognition might be lost; do not invent audience results. Map old-to-new tokens, logos, typography, templates, URLs and asset references where applicable. Preserve previous source and approval records.

Define owners, rollout order, coexistence period, replacement inventory, fallback/rollback and handling of already printed or deployed work. No live replacement, deletion or publication follows from planning. Distinguish a temporary campaign extension from permanent identity change. A local refresh task must not silently rename the business, rewrite positioning or replace approved product marks.

## Output and acceptance

Change map, continuity comparison, migration checklist, owner/approval matrix and rollback plan.

Carry file versions, approval scope and unresolved checks through [stage handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md).
