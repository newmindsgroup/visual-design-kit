# Loop and transition design

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce loop and transition design within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `screen-storyboard` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Choose seamless or explicit-reset looping based on the concept. Account for position, velocity and lighting at the join. Hold key information and make the sequence understandable when entered midway. Include a stable fallback when playback is unavailable.

## Output

Looping export, editable timeline, join frames and hold schedule. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Watch at least three complete cycles and several entry points on actual playback; do not certify from still thumbnails. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.

Flash-safety acceptance follows [display evaluation](../design-display-evaluation/SKILL.md) and the [motion specification](../../templates/motion-spec.md): record the recognized method, thresholds and exact tested file. A casual preview does not satisfy that check.
