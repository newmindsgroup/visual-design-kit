# Display audio and captions

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-12T11:33:18-04:00

## Inputs

Read the [production workflow](../../SCREEN-PRODUCTION-WORKFLOW.md). Reuse accepted `screen-video` inputs, source versions, target specification and user scope. Dependencies require information, not restarting approved work. No fixture design is included.

## Method

Confirm whether sound is allowed and useful. Preserve full meaning without sound. Use licensed audio and a project-specific loudness requirement; avoid arbitrary universal targets. Align captions with speech, keep them clear of product copy and allow reading time. Provide silent and audio variants when required.

## Outputs

Audio/caption plan, editable timing and delivery variants. Use the [stage handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Hand off actual files, version, candidate/approved status, dependencies, failures and next owner. Proposed paths are not produced artifacts; no skill grants external activation, spending or credentials.

## Acceptance

Listen on intended playback, verify sync, caption accuracy, readability and silent comprehension. Never assume audible playback in retail. Record executed evidence separately from plans. Use the [example](../../examples/screen-production/README.md) only within its declared scope.
