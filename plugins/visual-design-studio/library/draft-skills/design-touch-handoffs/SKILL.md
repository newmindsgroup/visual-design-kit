# Phone and staff handoffs

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce phone and staff handoffs within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `touchscreen-design` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Define what transfers, its destination, expiry and consent. Prefer minimal non-identifying context. Never embed credentials or personal information in a visible QR. Provide staff assistance and a non-phone route. Distinguish displayed handoff from confirmed receipt.

## Output

Handoff flow, payload contract, expiry behavior and fallback copy. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Test expired links, offline phones, abandoned sessions and absence of prior-user information. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.
