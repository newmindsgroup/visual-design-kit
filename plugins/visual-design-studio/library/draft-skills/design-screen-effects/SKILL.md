# Screen visual effects

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce screen visual effects within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `screen-storyboard` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Choose effects that explain product qualities or support the concept. Composite liquid, particles or lighting without obscuring claims or inventing product performance. Separate effect passes and keep a clean baseline. Test representative frames before costly renders.

## Output

Editable effect layers or scene, clean baseline and preview. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Check edge integration, visual noise, label visibility and flash behavior throughout the render. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.

Flash-safety acceptance follows [display evaluation](../design-display-evaluation/SKILL.md) and the [motion specification](../../templates/motion-spec.md): record the recognized method, thresholds and exact tested file. A casual preview does not satisfy that check.
