# Identity

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-12T11:33:18-04:00

Project-local instruction draft. Manual selection does not install a tool or grant permissions.

## Inputs

Direction decision, name and naming status, existing approved assets, rights, intended sizes/media/languages and accessibility constraints. Reuse valid existing prerequisites; missing audience research follows the [shared audience rule](../../CAPABILITY-CONTRACTS.md), including its hold or specifically authorized provisional-exploration branch. Never self-authorize an exception.

## Method

When a reference or reuse decision is needed, use [reference direction](../design-reference-direction/SKILL.md). Reuse an existing accepted decision; this optional route does not force a new research phase.

Map the direction to visual principles before drawing. Explore distinct concepts with rationale tied to the brief. Specify primary mark, secondary arrangement, monochrome, minimum size, clear space and misuse examples. Define semantic palette roles, type hierarchy, imagery, iconography and layout rules. Preserve exact supplied marks unless alteration is authorized. Test identity at actual smallest/largest intended sizes and in relevant backgrounds. Record font and asset licensing separately from visual approval. Include export specifications and editable source requirements; a generated preview is not a vector master. Trademark clearance remains a separate human/legal task.

## Output contract

Fill [design work](../../templates/design-work.md), selecting the sections relevant to this capability. Include: Concept rationale, selected candidate, logo variants, color/type/layout tokens, usage rules, asset rights register, source/export matrix and open decisions. Refer to stable IDs and paths rather than pasting full research or personas.

## Acceptance

No invented logo dimensions; assets are traceable; contrast and legibility checks are recorded; concept approval and legal clearance are separate. Apply [quality boundaries](../../QUALITY.md). Unsupported checks remain pending with an owner and next action. For a revision, load [stage handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md) and compare only the authorized delta.

When logo-production, font-rights or naming questions are unresolved, route through the research capability. Verify the intended production requirements, exact font license and permitted uses, and naming evidence from appropriate authoritative sources. Record provenance and unresolved ownership or usage restrictions before dependent delivery. References inform original work; their marks and assets are not supplied project assets.
