# Product fidelity verification

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-12T11:35:16-04:00

## Inputs

Read the [production workflow](../../SCREEN-PRODUCTION-WORKFLOW.md). Reuse accepted `screen-compositing` inputs, source versions, target specification and user scope. Dependencies require information, not restarting approved work. No fixture design is included.

## Method

Compare output with authoritative approved product art. Check product name, flavor, size, quantity, legal label elements, silhouette and proportions. Inspect transformed and partially occluded frames as well as held frames. Automated OCR may flag differences but cannot approve packaging. Never invent hidden label faces.

## Outputs

Reference-to-output comparison and discrepancy log with affected frames. Hand off actual files, version, candidate/approved status, dependencies, failures and next owner. Proposed paths are not produced artifacts; no skill grants external activation, spending or credentials.

## Acceptance

Require actual source art and rendered output; if either is missing, do not claim fidelity. Preserve originals and correct the smallest affected layer. Record executed evidence separately from plans. Use the [example](../../examples/screen-production/README.md) only within its declared scope.

Complete the [stage handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md) with actual files, version, checks, unknowns and next owner.
