# Extreme-format composition

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce extreme-format composition within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `display-discovery` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Use actual pixel dimensions and visible region, not aspect ratio alone. Design ultrawide, narrow portrait and shelf strips independently. Choose focal zones and repetition intentionally; avoid shrinking a conventional ad into a strip. Keep meaningful words together without forcing overflow. Allow overscan margins only from device evidence.

## Output

Dimensioned zone map and compositions at native export size. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Check reading distance, product recognition, balanced type, clipping and safe zones; physical readability remains unverified until tested. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.

## Media quality extension

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T21:08:20-04:00

Before generation, map the focal product and reserved copy regions into the final screen aspect ratio. For narrow strips prefer a deliberately composed product/detail sequence over cropping a portrait poster. Prepare separate composition variants when a crop cannot preserve hierarchy. Inspect at native size and contextual scale; no stretched products or clipped required copy. Route asset planning through [generation art direction](../design-media-art-direction/SKILL.md).
