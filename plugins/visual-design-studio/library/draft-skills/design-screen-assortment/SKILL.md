# Product assortment storytelling

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T20:08:06-04:00

## Inputs and scope

Read the [advanced workflow](../../DISPLAY-ADVANCED-SKILLS.md). Reuse accepted `display-content` inputs, source versions, objective and constraints. Dependencies express information needs, not mandatory repeated work. Scope is screen content and interaction, not fixture design. Unknown facts remain unknown.

## Method

Group products by meaningful shopper choice, not available space. Define hero versus supporting products, consistent scale and category cues. Break dense assortments into readable sequences; preserve accurate product-to-message association.

## Deliverable and handoff

Assortment hierarchy and layout/sequence plan. Include exact files actually created, candidate status, assumptions, failed checks and next owner through the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Proposed paths are not produced files. No installed tool, publication authority or provider access is implied by this skill.

## Acceptance

Check variant identification, crowded cases and missing or changed products. Record observations separately from expected behavior. Missing runtime evidence remains pending; do not label plans or descriptions tested. Keep prior approved versions recoverable.
