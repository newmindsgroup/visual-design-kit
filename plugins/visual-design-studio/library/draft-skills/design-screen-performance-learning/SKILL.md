# Campaign performance learning

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T20:08:06-04:00

## Inputs and scope

Read the [advanced workflow](../../DISPLAY-ADVANCED-SKILLS.md). Reuse accepted `display-evaluation` inputs, source versions, objective and constraints. Dependencies express information needs, not mandatory repeated work. Scope is screen content and interaction, not fixture design. Unknown facts remain unknown.

## Method

Define the decision and available measurement before collecting data. Separate proof-of-play, attention, interaction and sales. Identify confounders such as placement, stock and promotion. Change a bounded variable when feasible; report uncertainty and privacy limits.

## Deliverable and handoff

Measurement plan, evidence-linked findings and revision hypothesis. Include exact files actually created, candidate status, assumptions, failed checks and next owner through the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Proposed paths are not produced files. No installed tool, publication authority or provider access is implied by this skill.

## Acceptance

Do not claim causal lift from uncontrolled comparisons or invent unavailable metrics. Record observations separately from expected behavior. Missing runtime evidence remains pending; do not label plans or descriptions tested. Keep prior approved versions recoverable.
