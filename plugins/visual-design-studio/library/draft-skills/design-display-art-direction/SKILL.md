# Display art direction

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T09:45:01-04:00

Status: authored project-local instructions; not installed or production-certified.

## Inputs

Read the relevant [display workflow](../../DISPLAY-WORKFLOW.md) and reuse accepted inputs from the [display brief](../../templates/display-design-brief.md). Dependencies identify information needed, not mandatory repetition of approved work. Preserve baseline locks for precise changes.

## Method

Reuse approved brand rules; propose supporting graphics without silently redesigning the identity. Compare distinct approaches using the same message and comparable content. Reuse creative-direction boards, typography, color and foundations methods. Show the flat artwork plus contextual mockups; label simulated environments and preserve the actual design geometry.

Explain relationships to architecture, merchandise, nearby signage and viewing angle. For multiscreen work map canvas coordinates, panel boundaries, seams, safe text zones, synchronization assumptions and independent-screen fallback. View-dependent 3D illusions need geometry/viewpoint evidence and an ordinary-view fallback; a perspective render is not installation proof.

Define image/illustration/character treatment and product fidelity. Do not invent texture, performance, packaging, proportions or product claims. Include brand recognizability and originality in critique, without treating minimalism or novelty as universal quality.

## Output and acceptance

Direction comparison, chosen/provisional direction, screen map and annotated in-context views. Verify reading order and important content clear of seams at intended angles.

Carry actual files, source versions, assumptions and remaining checks through the existing [stage handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md).

## Wall degradation

Record bezel compensation, panel failure and desynchronization behavior. Avoid placing essential facts across independently failing panels; specify an operator-supported fallback and test actual behavior before installation acceptance.
