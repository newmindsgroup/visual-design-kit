# Brand Strategy

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-08 15:36:02 AST

Project-local instruction draft. Manual selection does not install a tool or grant permissions.

## Inputs

Scoped brief, evidence register, stakeholder constraints, actor records or relevant complete personas selected under the [audience input rule](../../CAPABILITY-CONTRACTS.md#audience-input-selection), competitive alternatives and success criteria. Reuse valid existing prerequisites; missing audience research follows the shared rule's hold or specifically authorized provisional-exploration branch, never a self-declared exception.

## Method

Separate category observations from proposed positioning. Identify audience priority, problem, alternatives, credible differentiation and reasons to believe. Write a positioning statement with evidence IDs beside each factual claim. Compare at least two materially different directions against the same criteria, including an option to retain an effective baseline. Explain the recommendation and rejected tradeoffs. State what the brand should do, say and avoid across relevant touchpoints. Treat unsupported differentiation as a hypothesis requiring research, never as a verified market advantage.

## Output contract

Fill [design work](../../templates/design-work.md), selecting the sections relevant to this capability. Include: Positioning, audience priority, value proposition, reasons to believe, messaging hierarchy, direction alternatives, recommendation, risks and validation plan. Refer to stable IDs and paths rather than pasting full research or personas.

## Acceptance

Verify the recorded audience selection, rationale, evidence limits and named use under the shared audience input rule. Reassess affected audience inputs on scope, evidence or intended-use changes; preserve historical results and hold dependent use until required conditions are resolved. Audience records alone never prove usability or production acceptance.

Claims trace to current permitted sources; proposed values are labeled; direction serves a specific need; no unsupported superlatives; baseline changes are scoped. Apply [quality boundaries](../../QUALITY.md). Unsupported checks remain pending with an owner and next action. For a revision, load [stage handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md) and compare only the authorized delta.

## Writing continuation

Pass accepted positioning and message evidence to [content writing](../design-content-writing/SKILL.md) for verbal identity and channel execution. Reuse this strategy record; writing does not silently replace it.
