# Content playlist design

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T20:08:06-04:00

## Inputs and scope

Read the [advanced workflow](../../DISPLAY-ADVANCED-SKILLS.md). Reuse accepted `screen-loops` inputs, source versions, objective and constraints. Dependencies express information needs, not mandatory repeated work. Scope is screen content and interaction, not fixture design. Unknown facts remain unknown.

## Method

Define the purpose and order of spots, repeat frequency and total cycle. Respect actual player duration and transition rules. Balance repeated brand cues against fatigue; avoid conflicting offers. Expired or unavailable spots need valid replacements.

## Deliverable and handoff

Playlist manifest, transitions and scheduling assumptions. Include exact files actually created, candidate status, assumptions, failed checks and next owner through the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Proposed paths are not produced files. No installed tool, publication authority or provider access is implied by this skill.

## Acceptance

Inspect cycle boundaries, missing spots, audio continuity where relevant and expiry behavior. Record observations separately from expected behavior. Missing runtime evidence remains pending; do not label plans or descriptions tested. Keep prior approved versions recoverable.
