# Touch product discovery

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce touch product discovery within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `touchscreen-design` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Model the actual shopper decision and available product data. Choose browsing, search, filtering or comparison to fit it. Define empty results, unavailable items, incomplete data and staff help. Do not invent inventory freshness or product recommendations.

## Output

Task flows, content/data contract and annotated discovery screens. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Exercise no results, comparable attributes, stale availability and backtracking without losing context. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.

## Search and comparison content models

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T20:08:06-04:00

Define authoritative product attributes, units, synonyms and missing-value semantics. Specify filter combinations, sorting and comparable attributes. Missing information is not a negative product feature. Test products with unequal data coverage. See [advanced workflow](../../DISPLAY-ADVANCED-SKILLS.md).
