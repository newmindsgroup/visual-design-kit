## Produce and hand off

Fill [the content extension](../../templates/content-work.md) within the existing work record, not a competing registry. Agree the decision and content brief before substantial drafting; do not ask again for facts or authority already supplied. Outline before long drafts, compare meaningful alternatives when exploration is requested, then write specific copy using source-backed claims. Keep original observations, quotations, proposed language and unknowns distinct.

Review facts, audience/task fit, client voice, clarity, repetition and reading aloud. Remove generic filler and manufactured enthusiasm; do not manufacture testimonials, scarcity, numbers, lived experience or citations. Engaging language is appropriate where it helps; errors and sensitive decisions need direct clarity. No promise of undetectable AI writing.

Check actual character limits and rendered fit when applicable, plus variables, destination actions and accessibility. A text review does not prove implemented behavior, usability, rankings or conversion. Send the versioned approved-use copy and remaining holds to the named next consumer through the existing stage handoff and delivery process. Keep content approval separate from design approval and publication authority.

For regulated or high-consequence claims in any guide, identify the qualified review owner and applicable requirements. Hold dependent adoption when required review or evidence is missing; a stylistic review does not clear the claim.
