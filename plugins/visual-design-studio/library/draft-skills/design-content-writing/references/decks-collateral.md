# Pitch decks and marketing or sales collateral

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-08 15:53:18 AST

Use with the content skill for writing and designing a presentation or supporting material. These are original authoring heuristics, not a mandatory slide formula. Reuse accepted brand, audience, offer and evidence records. Select only what serves the audience's next decision.

## Define the communication job

Record recipient and decision role, familiarity and objections, desired decision, meeting or reading context, time available, delivery channel, language and accessibility needs, confidential versus shareable material, and the actual ask. Distinguish an investor pitch, sales presentation, partnership pitch, internal business case and product demonstration. Do not ask again for supplied context.

| Purpose | Candidate narrative building blocks, selected by relevance |
| --- | --- |
| Investor pitch | Customer problem, solution and product, market evidence and sizing method, alternatives, business model, traction, distribution, team, financial assumptions, funding ask and intended use |
| Sales presentation | Buyer situation, cost or consequence of the problem, desired outcome, proposed solution, differentiation and proof, delivery approach, commercial terms if authorized, objections and next step |
| Partnership pitch | Shared opportunity, complementary roles, mutual value, responsibilities, operating model, evidence, proposed terms and decision sought |
| Internal business case | Decision, alternatives including retaining the baseline, costs and assumptions, expected benefits, risks, implementation and accountable owner |
| Product demonstration | User job, realistic scenario, product sequence, observed result, limitations and next action; distinguish live behavior from mockup or roadmap |

Mark unknown traction, market size, pricing, terms and forecasts unknown. Separate actual results from projections; document calculation inputs, period, units and scenarios when numbers are used. Route material financial or specialist claims to qualified review. Never invent logos, customers, testimonials or endorsements. StoryBrand may help a customer narrative but does not replace financial evidence or every deck's structure.

## Write the story before polishing slides

Draft a narrative outline with the decision each section supports. Establish the central message, evidence and ask, then identify gaps and unnecessary sections. Choose the sequence based on audience and purpose; do not force a fixed slide count. Sketch content and visual structure together before high-fidelity design.

For each slide or page, record in the existing content work record:

- Stable slide/page ID and linked message, audience need and claim IDs.
- Intended takeaway, exact headline and on-slide copy.
- Evidence source, date and actual versus estimated status for claims and charts.
- Visual job: explain a sequence, show a comparison, demonstrate a product or support a claim. Specify the proposed diagram, chart, screenshot or image and its data/asset source.
- Speaker notes where relevant, transition to the next slide, and any timing target based on the actual presentation allowance.
- CTA or decision sought where applicable, confidentiality/use status, and any appendix reference.

A presenter-led deck can use sparse slides and fuller notes. A standalone deck must make its argument without an absent speaker; add context or create a separately versioned leave-behind. Keep both versions linked to one canonical claim/message source. Do not hide essential qualifications only in notes or an unreadable footer.

## Select supporting collateral

| Artifact | Content that makes it useful | Next consumer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-pager / capability statement | Audience problem, concise offer, credible differentiators, proof and next step | Sales or introduction recipient |
| Brochure / product sheet | Benefits, appropriate specifications, use cases, conditions and contact/action | Prospect comparing fit |
| Case study / customer story | Context, supported baseline, intervention, measured outcome and limits; permissions | Prospect evaluating evidence |
| Proposal / scope of work | Client need, proposed scope, deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities, schedule, assumptions and authorized terms | Decision owner and contracting review |
| Sales battlecard / objection guide | Internal audience, alternatives, supported comparisons, discovery questions, responses and escalation | Sales team; label internal-only where applicable |
| Pricing / comparison sheet | Current authorized prices, units, inclusions, conditions, source date and fair comparison basis | Commercial decision-maker |
| Event / launch kit | Core message, audience/channel variants, invitation or handout, talking points and follow-up draft | Event or campaign team |
| Demo script / follow-up email | Scenario, demonstrated value, factual limitations, recap and agreed next step | Presenter or sales owner |

Use the editorial guide for case-study depth and the campaign guide for promotional channels. A proposal is not a signed contract. Keep internal battlecards and private pricing out of public packages. Preparing follow-up copy does not authorize sending it.

## Connect content to design and production

Use the CAMPAIGN pack with other packs only when needed. Handoff the selected slide/page inventory, exact copy, notes, source-linked chart data, asset requirements and intended viewing context to composition. Select software through SOFTWARE-PRODUCTION.md based on required editable source and recipient workflow. If an editable deck is required, a PDF or screenshots alone do not fulfill it; choose the actual supported slide tool and verify access and exports.

Preserve hierarchy and readable content at the intended viewing distance. Use meaningful chart units, labels, source dates and scales; do not make weak evidence seem strong through visual distortion. Provide meaningful image descriptions and inspect reading order and contrast in the actual output where applicable. Coordinate copy edits with charts, layouts, notes and related collateral through stable IDs.

## Review and handoff

Review argument continuity, audience fit, factual support, brand voice, consistency of figures/terms, clarity of ask and confidentiality. For a live deck, rehearse and measure pacing; for a standalone document, check that it can be understood without narration. Record actual checks and gaps, not presumed comprehension or sales performance.

Use the existing work record and delivery preflight for native editability/reopen, PDF or other export, font/media dependencies, chart rendering, links, notes visibility, accessibility and intended display checks as applicable. Name the canonical source, included variants, owner, version and update trigger. Keep content approval, design acceptance and authority to present, send or publish separate.
