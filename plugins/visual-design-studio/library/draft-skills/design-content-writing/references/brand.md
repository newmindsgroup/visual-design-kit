# Brand language

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-08 15:36:02 AST

Reuse positioning; resolve competing official rules with the owner. Produce only selected artifacts: message hierarchy, value proposition, elevator pitch, brand story, voice and tone guide, terminology/glossary, naming explorations, slogans or taglines. Distinguish an enduring brand tagline from a campaign slogan.

For voice, specify observable attributes, vocabulary, sentence patterns and paired on-brand/off-brand examples, adapted by audience and situation. Link each rule to official evidence or mark it proposed. For messages connect benefit, reason to believe and supporting source. Separate a brand aspiration from a current product promise.

For naming or tagline exploration, vary the strategic idea rather than merely synonyms. Compare clarity, relevance, differentiation, memorability, spoken use, localization and fit at the intended size. Explain the recommended option and tradeoffs. Original drafting is not proof of uniqueness or trademark availability; record screening scope and any specialist clearance needed before adoption. Do not label screening performed unless it was.

Use StoryBrand selectively as described in the shared workflow. Preserve the client's tone and substantiated claims. Handoff: voice/message IDs and selected language to website, UX, campaign and identity applications.
