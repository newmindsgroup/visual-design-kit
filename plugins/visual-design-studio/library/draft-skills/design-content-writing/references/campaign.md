# Campaign and sales copy

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-08 15:53:18 AST

Start from audience awareness, offer, evidence, channel, objective, CTA and destination. Select ads, social posts, captions, email subjects/preheaders/body, sales one-pagers, brochures, decks, scripts, signage or promotional variants as scoped. Preserve the core promise from message to destination.

Develop distinct angles tied to a real audience need and supported proof; compare clarity, voice, relevance and the requested action. For short formats define message hierarchy and actual placement limits. For displays record dwell/viewing conditions; for scripts separate spoken text, on-screen copy and captions. Do not fit long copy by silently shrinking type.

Record provenance for testimonials and proof, conditions for offers, factual expiry and any required disclosures. Do not invent endorsements, savings, scarcity or guarantees. Verify current platform specifications for the selected placement at production time. Legal and regulated claims need the appropriate owner; no blanket compliance claim.

Handoff: channel/variant IDs, exact copy and CTA destinations to composition and production. Keep an optional experiment hypothesis, success metric and comparison plan distinct from observed results. Sending, posting, spending and campaign activation require their own authority.

For presentations and supporting sales material, use [pitch decks and collateral](decks-collateral.md). It adds purpose-specific narratives, slide/page records, speaker notes, standalone versions and collateral selection while preserving the shared message and claim sources.
