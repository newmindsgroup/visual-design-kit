# Editorial and help content

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-08 15:36:02 AST

Select articles, resources, educational guides, case studies, help/FAQ entries, onboarding messages, lifecycle emails or release notes based on the reader's task and the project's scope. Define the question, audience knowledge, source needs, outline and next action. Reuse website or UX rules when those surfaces overlap.

Create useful original synthesis with attributed sources and concrete examples. Distinguish a synthetic example from an actual customer story. Case studies need supported baseline, intervention and measured result; do not imply causation from a coincidental change. Product instructions require the real version and actual behavior. Interview or book source material remains private unless its use and redistribution are authorized.

Record owner, update trigger, review date and archive/replacement proposal. Localization includes cultural fit and meaning, not only translation; identify the language reviewer where needed. Recheck facts and links, explain unfamiliar terms and remove repetition. Readability scores are diagnostics, not proof of comprehension.

Handoff: topic/article/help IDs, source links, approved copy, related content and maintenance conditions to the publication or support owner. A publication-ready draft is not a published page.
