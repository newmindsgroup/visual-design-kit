# Dimensional product visualization

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce dimensional product visualization within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `screen-assets` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Determine whether the job needs actual 3D or a limited 2.5D treatment. Use supplied geometry and label artwork where possible. Record unseen surfaces and do not invent them as accurate. Separate camera, material, light and product motion. Render representative frames before a full sequence.

## Output

Scene or layered depth project, camera/light notes and preview frames. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Check silhouette, label distortion, reflections and unseen-surface assumptions against approved evidence. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.
