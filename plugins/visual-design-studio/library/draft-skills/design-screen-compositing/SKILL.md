# Product compositing

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce product compositing within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `screen-assets` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Establish product scale, light direction, perspective and contact shadows before adding atmosphere. Match grain, sharpness and color between sources. Protect labels and product geometry; isolate treatment layers from approved product pixels. Use a suitable image editor rather than regenerating accurate packaging.

## Output

Layered composition with original product references and flat preview. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Compare packaging and proportions to originals; inspect masks and lighting at export size. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.

## Media quality extension

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T21:08:20-04:00

For material-specific product lighting, record the intended light source and surface response. Glass needs readable edge/reflection separation without invented contents; metal needs controlled gradients and reflections rather than blown highlights; food needs believable scale, freshness and texture without unsupported ingredient claims; textiles need coherent weave, drape and seams. These are starting hypotheses, not presets for every brand. Compare factual color/material against authorized references. Use the [reference-control skill](../design-media-reference-control/SKILL.md) and [repair skill](../design-media-image-repair/SKILL.md).
