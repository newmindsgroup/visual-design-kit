# Shelf-edge content design

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T20:08:06-04:00

## Inputs and scope

Read the [advanced workflow](../../DISPLAY-ADVANCED-SKILLS.md). Reuse accepted `screen-formats` inputs, source versions, objective and constraints. Dependencies express information needs, not mandatory repeated work. Scope is screen content and interaction, not fixture design. Unknown facts remain unknown.

## Method

Use actual strip dimensions and product position references. Keep text and imagery legible within very limited height. Distinguish decorative repetition from product-specific labels. Do not imply a price applies to adjacent merchandise. Screen content only, no shelf engineering.

## Deliverable and handoff

Strip zone map and native-size content variants. Include exact files actually created, candidate status, assumptions, failed checks and next owner through the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Proposed paths are not produced files. No installed tool, publication authority or provider access is implied by this skill.

## Acceptance

Check product association, repeated segments, cropping and readable type in context. Record observations separately from expected behavior. Missing runtime evidence remains pending; do not label plans or descriptions tested. Keep prior approved versions recoverable.
