# Product and graphic animation

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T19:51:13-04:00

## Objective and inputs

Produce product and graphic animation within the user's screen-content scope. Read the [screen workflow](../../SCREEN-CONTENT-SKILLS.md) and reuse approved upstream files, brief, source versions and constraints. The catalog dependency `screen-storyboard` identifies required information, not a mandatory rerun. Missing inputs are explicit assumptions or scoped holds.

## Method

Define motion roles and a small consistent vocabulary of easing, amplitude and tempo. Separate product and background motion so attention has a clear owner. Preserve silhouette and labels through transforms. Use native editable timelines or suitable code, recording dependencies.

## Output

Editable animation and review frames. Carry source version, candidate status, editable file location, dependencies, limitations and next owner in the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Choose an available tool suited to the artifact; a skill does not establish authentication, paid allowance or native editing capability.

## Acceptance

Inspect starts/stops, label fidelity, stutter and attention competition; review rendered motion for flashing. Record actual evidence and failures. Never label an unexecuted check passed. Maintain the approved baseline until the candidate is accepted.

## Media quality extension

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T21:08:20-04:00

Separate camera motion, subject motion and graphic overlays in every shot instruction. Specify start/end framing, protected product details, motion amplitude and stable message hold. Inspect full playback for texture crawl, flicker, shape morphing, identity drift and abrupt speed changes. Prefer deterministic animation of exact packshots/logos where generation drifts. Record join-frame and timing checks through the [quality rubric](../../templates/media-quality-rubric.md).

Flash-safety acceptance follows [display evaluation](../design-display-evaluation/SKILL.md) and the [motion specification](../../templates/motion-spec.md): record the recognized method, thresholds and exact tested file. A casual preview does not satisfy that check.
