# Product configurators and guided selection

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-11T20:08:06-04:00

## Inputs and scope

Read the [advanced workflow](../../DISPLAY-ADVANCED-SKILLS.md). Reuse accepted `touch-product-discovery` inputs, source versions, objective and constraints. Dependencies express information needs, not mandatory repeated work. Scope is screen content and interaction, not fixture design. Unknown facts remain unknown.

## Method

Define option compatibility, required choices and business rules from authoritative data. Explain unavailable combinations and allow undo. Separate suggestions from factual compatibility. Summarize selected configuration and expose price/availability uncertainty.

## Deliverable and handoff

Decision tree, option constraints and selection summary specification. Include exact files actually created, candidate status, assumptions, failed checks and next owner through the [handoff](../design-stage-handoff/SKILL.md). Proposed paths are not produced files. No installed tool, publication authority or provider access is implied by this skill.

## Acceptance

Exercise incompatible options, reset, changed upstream choices and missing pricing. Record observations separately from expected behavior. Missing runtime evidence remains pending; do not label plans or descriptions tested. Keep prior approved versions recoverable.
