# Design progression site output contract

Version-Timestamp: 2026-10-03T12:31:00.335655-04:00

Use for requested redesign or scratch logo/identity review hubs. Fill from preserved artifacts and observed events. Default local/draft; do not invent discovery, experiments, human effort, elapsed time, reviews or acceptance to suggest a richer process. This contract specifies an output, not an implemented site or backend.

## Scope, evidence and endpoint

- Site ID/version/timestamp, project record and authoritative index reference, intended audience, owner, actual local/draft/authorized-hosted state and access evidence. External navigation uses safe relative/public approved locators, never internal machine paths.
- Starting condition: original supplied source/control for redesign, or actual brief/first artifact for scratch work. State rights, original source crop boundary, missing bytes/history and authorized reconstruction labels. Do not invent an original logo for scratch identity.
- Evidence-backed discovery, source/reference learning, concise decisions and controlled hypotheses. Identify unavailable or unreviewed sources. Attribute tools/AI and observable contributions accurately; show concise approved summaries rather than raw internal reasoning, reviewer prompts/logs or private book content.
- Identity endpoint: agreed deliverable scope and current phase. Logo selection, lettering, variants, system applications and delivery checks have separate statuses; state where the current identity work ends and what remains.

## Information architecture

Latest work first: current-working revision, feedback-ready options, focused question and limits. Group options into named families with stable option anchors. Provide compare and fullview of actual artifacts, original controls and matched-scale context; preserve intentional scale differences with clear labels. Chronological history remains available behind the latest view and includes every preserved produced iteration, experiments, failed methods, rejected/superseded archive and actual feedback/decisions. Do not hide rejection or imply reconstructed history is original. Missing produced revisions have explicit gap entries.

Selection states (rejected, superseded, current, accepted-for-stated-scope) are distinct from critique completion, criterion evidence, user visual acceptance and production readiness. Bind each disposition to the exact shown revision and scope; praise is not approval. Rejected treatments remain in the archive and cannot become recommendations through renaming/re-export. Authorized descendants retain parent/rejection lineage and pending acceptance. If faithful asset exploration was requested, a generic substitute stays labeled as a substitute and cannot satisfy that track.

Fidelity class per row: native-derived vector, raster hypothesis, raster companion with conversion limit, authorized reconstruction, or missing-bytes gap. A gap row has its own stable ID, expected parent (or unknown), missing artifact, reason/evidence and reconstruction ID if any; it cannot carry a fabricated preview or inherited acceptance.

## Artifact and rendering truth

Where native vector evidence exists, present approved SVG exports containing actual geometry, not raster images disguised as SVG or paths invented from a preview. Verify viewBox, transforms, compound cuts/fill rules, stroke/painted bounds and appearance against the same native/export revision at intended sizes and reversed use. Preserve raster hypotheses as clearly labeled sketches. For formats/exports that cannot preserve appearance, show a verified raster companion and the conversion limit; never call the website a native editable master. Crops/detail enlargements retain their source revision and boundary. Shared anchor equality alone does not prove optical consistency.

## Feedback and collaboration behavior

- Each option has a stable shareable anchor within the authorized site and a copy-feedback reference containing site version, revision/option ID and criterion/question. Local anchors are not externally accessible links.
- Without a durable backend, show that copy-feedback reference and direct users to an authorized feedback channel. Do not claim saved comments, team presence, live sync or cross-device persistence. If temporary in-browser controls exist, label their storage/loss behavior accurately. Temporary notes do not become verified feedback events or acceptance states until the executor records a verified response.
- With authorized implemented persistence, verify access, exact revision binding, save/reload and second-device behavior before claiming those features. Publication, permission changes and external sending remain separately authorized.
- The project executor appends verified responses received through the authorized channel to the authoritative index as events. The snapshot hub never writes to that index; no-backend feedback output is the copy-reference only. The hub presents a snapshot; identify its refresh time and stale/unresolved responses.

## External-site boundary and acceptance checklist

Keep raw internal review logs/prompts, confidential references, private books, secrets and machine paths out of the external artifact and its downloadable files/source/bundled data. Review summaries and selected provenance are included only for the authorized audience. A hidden UI section is not access control. For confidential collaboration, verify server-side access before sharing. Record the exact authorized hosting workflow/tool and destination when selected. Until then, hosting route is unspecified and blocked; this template does not invoke a Sites capability or invent a hosting system.

- Reconcile the hub inventory to the authoritative index: preserved produced revisions, explicit gaps, immutable lineage and truthful current pointer.
- Verify feedback/status against actual evidence, rejected archive, source fidelity and endpoint limits. Test that no accepted claim is inferred from a completed critique or structural equality.
- Inspect actual SVG/raster outputs and desktop/mobile renders. Exercise anchors, compare/fullview, keyboard/focus, zoom and copy-feedback behavior; report unsupported interactions and inaccessible assets. Check accessibility through the selected implementation workflow.
- Exclude kit-internal provenance/hostname strings as well as machine paths from filled output. Inspect exported source/bundles/downloads for excluded private material and verify access on the actual share destination when authorized.
- Record actual checks/reviewer/model, holds and audience scope. Template completion or local rendering does not prove hosting, durable collaboration, user acceptance or production readiness.
