## Renderer prompt to fill once

```text
Role: Render one bounded logo experiment from the supplied concept and verified
attachments. The concept author owns the idea. Do not invent brand requirements.

Objective: [candidate/parent ID]. Test [original shape/lettering mechanism] so
[specific intended meaning and visible effect]. This is [concept raster/native
study/authorized refinement], with [named next decision].

Context: [known audience/purpose/uses]. Exact complete name: [text including case
and diacritics]. Baseline: [approved source ID/version]. Reference images are
attached in [verified slots/IDs]. Learn [region and mechanism per reference];
avoid copying [protected or irrelevant forms]. Coverage gaps: [actual gaps].

Requirements: Preserve [locked structure/equity]. Follow [attributed preference
IDs]. Exclude [visible structural vetoes]. Construct [silhouette/counter/join/
terminal/proportion experiment]. For a typography problem, solve the required
letter structure before decorative integration. Fail if [observable defect].
Typography scope: [fixed source or separately authorized whole-name exploration].
Source: [exact source/specimen/native construction ID and parameters]. [Use pinned lettering
unchanged, or omit generated text for deterministic assembly from that source].
Do not substitute or regenerate locked lettering.

Output: [authorized format/dimensions/background]. Follow preview recipe [ID].
Show complete-name shallow horizontal and stacked lockups and standalone small
single-color mark [actual dimensions]. For wordmark-only, use the complete name
as the standalone mark and add no icon. Stack at authorized word boundaries;
record stacked as not applicable for an indivisible name and use a separate
compact full-name view, keeping its letters intact. Do not call that view stacked. Keep scale, margins and placement matched.
If this renderer cannot produce a required view or exact typography, output only
[authorized partial asset] for local assembly; report the missing part.

Evaluation: Deliver [actual files]. Report deviations and rendering limits.
The author will inspect the clean pixels against [frozen criteria] before
presentation. Output success is not user approval or production readiness.
```
