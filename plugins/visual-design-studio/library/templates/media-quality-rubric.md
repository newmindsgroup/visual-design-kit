# Media quality rubric

Parent: [workflow](../MEDIA-QUALITY-WORKFLOW.md). Owner: [evaluation](../draft-skills/design-media-quality-evaluation/SKILL.md).

## Reject before scoring

Incorrect product geometry, labels or required facts; unauthorized source use; cropped mandatory content; corrupt output; unsupported delivery dimensions; unreviewed flashing or hazardous motion. Mark a relevant criterion unknown when evidence is missing. Unknown required evidence prevents final acceptance.

## Evidence-based comparison

Score each dimension 1 to 5: 1 has a named major failure; 3 meets the brief with specific repairs remaining; 5 meets the brief with no observed defect in the inspected scope. Scores 2 and 4 are intermediate and need reasons. Attach image region/frame references; no overall score overrides a failed hard requirement.

| Dimension | Inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Composition | Focal point, hierarchy, intentional space, balance and crop |
| Brand fit | Reference-backed type, color, imagery and tone; meaningful distinction |
| Craft | Masks, perspective, light, material realism, edge quality and artifacts |
| Readability | Exact copy, line balance, contrast, size and message hold |
| Coherence | Consistency across assets, formats, characters and shots |
| Motion when applicable | Full playback, geometry stability, rhythm, transitions and loop join |

Compare candidates at equal display size with identical evaluation scope. For stills inspect native pixels and reduced/contextual views. For motion retain playback evidence plus representative and failure frames. Accessibility/device testing remains separately recorded.

Decision: candidate accepted for next stage, repair, reject or held for evidence. List top three repairs, their owner, and which checks repeat. Human preference and real device acceptance remain distinct from automated or model review.


## Photographic brief hard requirements

For product/environment photography, visibly schematic substitutes and implausible material, geometry, reflection, shadow or scene integration are rejection conditions, regardless of average score. Check full frame, native-resolution details and contextual size. Exact packaging/product identity requires approved references; generated plausibility alone is insufficient.

Record photographic appearance, product truth, brand fit, technical delivery and human acceptance separately. Keep missing evidence explicit. Approved illustrative briefs and vector typography/diagrams are not required to look photographic. A dark palette or added texture is not evidence of quality. Carry a rejected still's hold into dependent motion, adaptations and fallback assets.

### Scope and decision combination

Apply photographic criteria to regions intended to depict photographed products or environments, not symbolic icons, diagrams, editable text or logo artwork. A photographic product with a vector label must still have plausible label perspective and material integration. Record an explicitly authorized alternative medium in the production packet with the user/client instruction reference and its scope; do not infer approval from an agent's own suggestion. A user's explicit instruction can supply this authority.

For each stage, declare required criteria and evidence before judging. Any required rejection means reject; otherwise any required unresolved evidence means held; otherwise any repair means repair; only satisfied required criteria permit acceptance for that stated stage. Not-applicable criteria require a reason. Held and rejected baselines cannot advance dependent production motion or adaptations. Internal candidate review does not imply human approval. Where human acceptance is required, absent approval keeps release held. Record the exact artifact hash and authority for each accepted scope.

The project owner declares required criteria and human approval stages in the production packet or handoff before work begins. Record the authority and instruction reference. For client/team release, human acceptance is required unless that owner explicitly documents a different release authority. Internal agent review never supplies client approval. Non-required criteria are reported as advisory findings and cannot silently waive required criteria.

A hard-requirement failure is always reject regardless of numeric score. On other required dimensions, a score of 1 means reject; 2 or 3 means repair. A 4 or 5 alone cannot establish acceptance: named outstanding repairs must be resolved and required evidence present. Missing approved product references means held, not a fabricated factual rejection. Pure vector information graphics need a photographic-scope N/A reason, not permission to use vector artwork.
