# Visual-language family specification

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-09 10:37:21 AST

A linked attachment to design-work owned by identity for approved brand choices. Reuse canonical records rather than editing a second token store. This template accounts for categories without requiring every category to be produced.

## Context and applicability

Record/version, owner, brand starting state, selected direction and actual approval scope, audience/context, baseline assets/tokens, campaign versus permanent brand scope, output channels and rights constraints.

For each family record create/reuse/extend/not-applicable/hold, rationale, upstream record, owner and next consumer: logo/lockups; typography; palette; photography; illustration; characters/mascots; icons/symbols; shapes/containers; patterns; textures/materials; composition/layout; UI surfaces/states; data graphics/infographics; motion/animation; channel-specific assets including ads, sales collateral and physical displays. Follow existing specialized contracts for selected families.

## Repeat for each selected family

- Stable family/asset IDs, role and meaning, approved reference paths/versions and source rights.
- Link the authoritative design-work family approval: proposed, accepted for named campaign, accepted for permanent brand scope, rejected or superseded; identify the actual approving party and evidence. The identity capability maintains the record but does not self-approve client choices. Promotion from campaign to permanent requires a new explicit owner decision and affected guideline/token/asset updates with new versions.
- Construction rules: geometry/proportions, edges/strokes, perspective, detail, light/shadow, material/texture and palette/token references as applicable.
- Relationship rules: type/image balance, hierarchy, spacing, scale, contrast, proximity, figure/background and consistency with other families.
- Invariants versus permitted variation; campaign/channel exceptions, authorized owner and expiry/review trigger.
- Correct and incorrect visual examples with annotations, crop-safe regions, minimum-use conditions and context-specific requirements.
- Required editable assets and outputs, sizes/states/backgrounds/languages, canonical master, export naming/version and dependencies.
- Actual inspection evidence, drift/rejection criteria and unresolved limitations. An exemplar does not prove all variants.

## Character system, when selected

Character ID/name and narrative role; personality expressed through behavior without audience stereotypes; species/body construction; silhouette and proportion map; face/feature placement; distinctive marks; palette/material; costume/accessories; scale relationships across the cast. Separate immutable identity traits from permitted pose/expression/wardrobe variants.

Provide applicable front/side/back/three-quarter views, expression sheet, key poses, hands/props interactions, silhouette and small-size tests. Specify perspective, lighting, line/shading conventions and motion/acting rules. Record what is not yet depicted rather than inventing unseen anatomy. Include multi-character scenes and approved object/character size relationships when used. Preserve likeness permissions and asset rights; fictional character designs cannot borrow another brand's recognizable character identity without authorization.

Generation packet: exact reference sheet versions, requested pose/view/emotion, allowed edits, scene/camera/light/crop, excluded changes, selected tool/model settings if available and output destination. Compare face, proportions, distinctive traits, costume, palette and style with the approved sheet. Seeds alone are not consistency evidence.

## System stress check and change control

Select representative normal and difficult uses: small/large, monochrome, light/dark background, dense/sparse composition, narrow/physical screen, print, localization, motion or multi-character scene as applicable. Record the artifact/version and actual result per test. Inspect family resemblance without requiring identical compositions. Propose corrections to the authoritative owner; invalidate affected tests after changes.

Hand off the selected specification, canonical assets/tokens and revision rules to production. Approval, artifact integrity, creative quality, accessibility and native-tool verification remain separate statuses.
