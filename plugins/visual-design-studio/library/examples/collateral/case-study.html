The challenge

Volume had doubled in four years, but routes and shifts were still drawn by hand each week. One in six deliveries arrived late, and overtime had become the default way to catch up.

What we did

We measured a month of real stops, rebuilt the routes around loading times and traffic, and matched shift starts to hourly volume. A four-week pilot in one region proved the plan before rollout.

Approach

  1. Weeks 1-3

    Map

    Depot visits, ride-alongs and four weeks of telematics data cleaned into one model.

  2. Weeks 4-7

    Model

    Three network options compared on cost, service level and risk with the operations team.

  3. Weeks 8-12

    Pilot and hand over

    One region ran the new plan for four weeks; then training and a playbook for the planners.

The plan looks like something our own dispatchers would have drawn, if they had had a month without phones ringing.

Dana Okafor, Operations Director, Alder & Finch Freight (fictional person)

Results

  • Late deliveries down from 16% to 12.6% across all three depots.
  • Overtime down by a third without adding headcount.
  • Weekly planning time cut from two days to half a day.

What comes next

The client's planners now update the model each quarter. A fourth depot opens in 2027 and will start on the same plan from day one.
