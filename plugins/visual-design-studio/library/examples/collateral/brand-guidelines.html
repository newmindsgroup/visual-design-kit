How to use this book

Start with the logo and color pages; most mistakes happen there. Every value in this book comes from the brand tokens, so the website, the slides and the print files stay the same color and type. When this book and a vendor spec disagree, the vendor spec wins for that job; tell the brand team.

01

Logo

A single-color mark and a wordmark. Use the full lockup; use the mark alone only where the name already appears nearby, such as an app icon or a social avatar.

Files: logo-primary, logo-reversed and logo-one-color as SVG (screen), PDF (print, outlined) and PNG at 1x and 2x. Slot: supply the outlined artwork; the live-text wordmark in this sample is a stand-in.

Clear space and size

Keep clear space of x on every side, where x is half the height of the mark. Nothing else enters that space: no text, no edges, no other logos.

xx MeridianEmber

Placement

  • Top left or bottom left on most layouts; centered only on covers and signage.
  • Lockup width about one fifth of the page width on letter and A4 covers.
  • Keep it inside the safe zone: at least 5mm from any trimmed edge.

05

Imagery

  • Grade warm and clean: no heavy filters, no crushed blacks. Keep skin tones true.
  • Behind text, use a two-tone version (ink and primary) or a calm area of the photo, then check 4.5:1 for the text.
  • Print needs 300 ppi at the placed size; ask the photographer for CMYK conversions to the printer's profile.
  • Avoid stock handshakes, staged high fives and laptops on beaches.
  • Do not present AI-generated people as customers or staff.
  • Record the source, license and release for every image in the asset register.

06

Voice

We sound like a senior colleague who has done the work before: plain, specific and calm.

Plain

Short sentences, common words, active verbs.

We sayWe cut delivery time by a fifth.

NotWe leverage synergies to optimize delivery outcomes.

Specific

Numbers, names and dates instead of adjectives.

We sayYour plan is ready on 14 March.

NotYour world-class plan is coming soon.

Calm

Confident without hype; no exclamation marks in headlines.

We sayHere is what changes, and what does not.

NotGet ready for a total game-changer!

Words we use

plan, team, deliver, clear, measured, on time, together

Words we avoid

leverage, synergy, seamless, world-class, unlock, disrupt
