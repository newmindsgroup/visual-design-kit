The temptation in quiet minimalism is light grey text on off-white. Here the lightest text color is usuzumi at 5.67:1; anything paler is a rule or a lattice, never words. Vermilion is the seal and nothing else: a second red element would turn calm into alarm.

07

Imagery and icons 写真と図像

Photography is natural light only, shot in the hour after sunrise or before dusk: paper, wood grain, steam, a single cup. People appear from behind or as hands. Never a wide-angle lens on a small room; never a guest posing in a yukata.

Icons are 1px hairlines on a 24px grid, square ends, drawn like architectural plan symbols. Texture is washi fiber and shadow, never a pattern of cherry blossoms, torii or Mount Fuji.

09

Voice 言葉

A host who speaks softly and precisely. Japanese in polite, unhurried teineigo; English plain and specific. Fewer adjectives than you think, and never "luxury".

Headline

庭の音だけが、聞こえる宿。

An inn where you hear only the garden.

A sensory promise rather than a claim. The English is transcreated, not translated word for word.

CTA

Asks, does not push. "Book now" would be the only urgent sentence on the site.

ご希望の日程は満室です。前後の空いている日をご案内します。

Apologetic tone in Japanese, practical in English; both move straight to alternatives.

10

Rationale 理由

Why this direction, its risks, and what the category would have done by default.

Why it fits

  • At eight rooms, the product is the atmosphere. A screen of light and shadow sells silence better than any photo carousel.
  • Innocent archetype: purity and simplicity, expressed with real materials rather than pastel clichés.
  • Japanese guests read vertical Mincho as cultured and sincere; international guests read the same restraint as "authentic Kyoto" without needing a single torii.

What it risks

  • Quiet can tip into empty or generic "Japandi". The shadow, the floor plan and the seal must stay; they are the owned assets.
  • Photography quality becomes critical. A weak photo has nowhere to hide on a page this calm.
  • Vertical text needs careful reading order and must never hold information that is not also in the horizontal text.

Category-expected alternative

A booking-engine template: full-width photo carousel, star ratings, an orange "Book now" button, discount banners and a long amenities grid (style.corporate-modern + tier.mid-market). It converts impulse travelers and cheapens an eight-room inn.

Confirm before launch: Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) for guest data, the Hotel Business Act guest-register requirements (passport details for foreign guests), and accessibility of the booking engine.
