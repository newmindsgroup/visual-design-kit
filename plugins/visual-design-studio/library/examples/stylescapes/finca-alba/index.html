03 · Tintas / InksPalette

Three printing inks (café, maga red, dawn gold) on two papers (kraft and crema), plus Cordillera green for the espresso line. Specified as spot colors for print; ratios computed with WCAG 2 relative luminance for the web store.

PrintSpecify as three spot inks plus kraft; match to physical ink drawdowns on the actual kraft and label stock, not to screen values.

No flag colorsThe red is the flor de maga (Puerto Rico's national flower), not the flag. The flag stays off the pack; its colors carry political meaning on the island.

Gold is never texton crema or kraft. It is rays, rules and fills behind café-colored type.

07 · Grabado / ImageryImagery and icons

Everything looks cut from linoleum and printed in at most three inks: chunky shapes, carved white lines, slightly uneven edges.

Commission a Puerto Rican printmaker for the master illustrations (sunrise, ridges, branch) and build the icon set from their carving marks. Photography is documentary: the actual pickers, hands, mud, the drying patio at 6 a.m. in warm low light. No beach, no flag, no generic "tropical" palm.

Icons are solid silhouettes with two to four carved highlights each, never outlines. Red is reserved for the fruit and the flower; gold for light.

10 · Razón / RationaleRationale

Why this direction fits, what it risks, and the default the category would reach for.

Why it fits

  • Premium coffee is judged on provenance. Heritage label grammar (badges, bands, stamped dates) reads as "small, real, specific" at a $20+ price.
  • Creator archetype: the linocut, the handwritten notes and the stamped lot are visible handwork, not decoration.
  • Localizes Americana to Puerto Rico: maga red, the Cordillera, bilingual labels, Spanish first.

What it risks

  • Americana can drift into mainland cowboy nostalgia; every motif must come from the island and the farm.
  • Heritage cues invite fake history. No "Est." date unless it is true.
  • Two languages on a 12 oz front get crowded; keep the front to name, origin, roast, notes and net weight, and move the rest to the back.

Category-expected alternative

A matte black specialty pouch with a small sans-serif sticker (third-wave minimalism), or a tourist pack with the flag, a coquí and a beach (style.quiet-minimal or souvenir kitsch). One is anonymous, the other is not respectful.

Before print: confirm FDA food labeling and the Fair Packaging and Labeling Act net-quantity statement (both units, bottom 30% of the main panel), bilingual consistency, origin claims and any certification marks with a regulatory specialist.
