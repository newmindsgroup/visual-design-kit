Section 5

Components

Every control is at least 48 by 48 pixels; main actions are 56 to 64 high and full width in the app. Labels sit above fields, never inside them.

Buttons

Focus: yellow fill and a 4px ink underline (the GOV.UK pattern). It shows on any background and does not rely on color perception alone.

Text input

It is on your pill bottle label, after “Rx”. For example, 4417-2209. Enter all 8 numbers from your pill bottle, like 4417-2209.

Section 7

Imagery and icons

Show the medicine, not a mood. Real pictures of each pill help people check they are taking the right one.

Photography shows real older adults at home in daylight, doing ordinary things: a kitchen counter, a weekly pill box, a phone held at arm’s length. Ages, bodies and skin tones vary. Never use smiling stock seniors in white, stethoscopes, or glowing medical holograms.

Icons are simple 2.5px outlines with round ends on a 28px grid, always next to a word. No icon appears alone as a button.

Section 9

Voice

A patient pharmacist on the phone: warm, exact, unhurried. Use the person’s words (“medicine”, “pill”), give the next step every time, and never blame.

Section 10

Rationale

Why plain beats pretty for this audience.

Why it fits

People over 65 often manage several medicines with changing eyesight, dexterity and memory, and many distrust apps that hide things. Patterns from public services (GOV.UK, USWDS) were tested with exactly these users: large labels above fields, one task per screen, obvious focus, words before icons.

The Caregiver archetype shows up as reassurance and shared care, not as soft pastel imagery.

What it risks

  • It can look like a government form. Warmth comes from the voice, the greeting by name and the pill pictures.
  • Families and pharmacists see health data: HIPAA, consent for sharing and text-message consent (TCPA) shape these flows. Confirm with counsel.
  • Platform text-size settings must scale all of this further; test at the largest iOS and Android sizes.

The expected alternative

A blue gradient with a stethoscope, a smiling stock couple, 14px gray text on white, icon-only tabs and swipe-to-confirm gestures. It looks modern in a pitch deck and fails the people who need it most.
