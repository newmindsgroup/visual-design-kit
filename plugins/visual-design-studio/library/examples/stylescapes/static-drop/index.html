03Palette

Black-led, one hot interrupter (safety orange), one cold one (ice), and chrome. No red-and-black: that is every punk cliché. Ratios computed with WCAG 2 relative luminance.

Errors use a separate red (#FF7A66 on black, 7.76:1; #B3200F on xerox, 5.38:1) so a failed payment never looks like a hype sticker. Orange on xerox is only 4.15:1 with #C2410C, so orange stays on black or as a fill under black text.

04Type

Anton shouts, roughed up with an SVG displacement filter so it looks photocopied twice. Unbounded is only the chrome logotype and prices. Courier Prime is everything you actually read: typewriter zine, still a clean text face.

07Imagery

Flash photography, photocopied twice, cut out with scissors. Then one clean product shot, because people still need to see the garment.

Lookbook: shot on a point-and-shoot with direct flash, at night, on real friends in real places (laundromats, rooftops, the last train). Cut-outs get a 4px xerox-white edge and sit on black. Product pages switch to plain, evenly lit flat lays on xerox grey so color and fabric are honest.

Icons are drawn like marker on tape: 2.5px strokes, square ends, a little displacement so no line is perfectly straight. Y2K enters through sparkles, chrome and old OS windows, never through purple gradients or fake 3D blobs.

08Motion

Hard cuts, not easing. Things jump between two or three frames like a cheap camcorder. Glitches are rare (one burst every few seconds), offset only, never a brightness flash.

10Rationale

Why this direction, what it risks, and what the category would do by default.

Why it fits

  • Drop culture runs on scarcity and screenshots. Xerox grit says "made by people", chrome says "collectible".
  • Teens read zine cut-and-paste and Y2K chrome as theirs, not as their parents' minimalism.
  • Outlaw archetype aimed at the beige, endlessly restocked mall brand, not at the customer.

What it risks

  • Texture can bury information. Rule: filters only on display type and art, never on prices, sizes, forms or body copy.
  • SVG filters cost CPU on old phones; render textures as images in production.
  • Hype mechanics aimed at minors can turn into pressure. No fake timers, no fake stock counts, no "3 people are looking".

Category-expected alternative

A clean black-and-white grotesk storefront with model photos on grey seamless and a standard theme layout (style.corporate-modern + style.swiss-international). Converts fine, has no reason to be screenshotted.

Check with counsel: FTC guidance on fake urgency and scarcity claims, COPPA if any user may be under 13, and SMS consent rules (TCPA) for drop alerts.
