## Gap ownership

Environment, distance and attention: discovery and evaluation. Brand continuity, multiscreen mapping and original product imagery: art direction. Product storytelling and balanced type: content. Orientation, languages, schedules, expired offers and stale data: campaigns. Reach, idle/privacy and uncertain transactions: touchscreen design. Motion identity, storyboards, animatics, loops, kinetic typography and compositing: shared motion extension. Editable source, real player limits, dependencies and rollback: production. Comprehension versus proof-of-play, accessible behavior and measured improvement: evaluation.

## Sources and limits

Consult current operator specifications for each actual job. [Broadsign screen fields](https://docs.broadsign.com/broadsign-reach/en/screens.html) illustrate duration requirements; they are not a universal player contract. [W3C accessibility reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/) informs applicable web-content checks; physical reach and installation approval remain separate.

## Checks to execute with a real artifact

Passive: join a loop midway, inspect silent playback, long copy, offline fallback and expired promotion. Touch: idle to task to reset, interrupted task, no hover, seated reach and help path. Wall: seam avoidance and loss of synchronization. Preserve failed evidence and corrections. These are test scenarios, not results.

## Routing and reuse rules

Content owns what is communicated and static hierarchy; motion owns timed choreography and animation assets; campaigns owns variants/data/scheduling. Production owns editable source and technical export checks; evaluation owns consolidated acceptance and comprehension evidence. One request may need several stages, but each artifact has one owner. A silent store loop starts with discovery, reuses accepted content, then motion and production; evaluation records results. A schedule-only revision selects campaigns and preserves artwork. A static poster does not require a timeline.

Accepted inputs must identify source version, scope and approval. Changed content, format or device invalidates affected checks only. Expired rights require withdrawal or a separately valid fallback under the campaigns expiry rules. Do not manufacture new personas or identity for an unchanged approved baseline.

Rights evidence belongs to discovery; expiry to campaigns; license packaging to production. Touchscreen owns physical-access design; evaluation consolidates results. Additional cases: flash analysis for every passive/wall loop, timezone/DST boundaries and unknown clock, timeout extension, prior-user data clearing and uncertain-operation recovery.
