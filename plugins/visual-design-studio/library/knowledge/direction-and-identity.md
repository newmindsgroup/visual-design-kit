# Build a direction that can become a visual family

Version-Timestamp: 2026-09-27 14:20:29 AST

Use when visual direction is open, supporting graphics lack coherence, or an approved identity needs a scoped extension. Inputs: brief, audience/context evidence, accepted brand assets, real content, output formats and allowed changes. This is original authored guidance; the miniature is fictional and not an approved identity.

## Make the decision

1. Turn broad attributes into an observable design job. “Precise” might mean clearly aligned measurements and restrained annotation for one brief. It does not automatically mean a geometric font, blue palette or empty page. Link the operation to a specific audience need or supplied brand rule.
2. Write a central idea that connects message and form. Then choose the smallest useful set of ingredients: composition, type character, image role, color relationship and supporting shape behavior. An idea should predict decisions across formats. A list of fashionable adjectives cannot do that.
3. Compare directions using the same content and representative surface. Distinct concepts change the organizing idea, not just the accent color. Annotate what each reference teaches, what will be original and what must not be copied. Name evidence for fit and what remains a hypothesis. Avoid matching industries to aesthetic stereotypes.
4. Choose the image medium by its communication obligation. Use verified photography or controlled product compositing when the image claims to depict a real product. Use vector graphics for an exact diagram or symbol. Illustration can simplify an abstract relationship. Photographic appearance and factual product fidelity are separate tests: a plausible generated bottle can still be the wrong bottle. Do not replace a photographic brief with a schematic render to avoid difficult craft.
5. After an actual direction decision, extract family rules from the successful application: proportions, edges, strokes, spacing, image viewpoint, light, material, detail, crops and relationship to type. Distinguish invariants from permitted variation. Too few rules produce drift; too many make every asset a duplicate.
6. Stress the family with a contrasting use: dense and sparse, small and large, monochrome and full color, or a second language as relevant. Change the construction rule if siblings fail together. Repair an individual asset if only it fails. Keep campaign-specific expression separate from permanent identity authority.

## Worked miniature

Fictional repair studio “Relay Workshop” has supplied evidence that customers want to see what is being repaired before approving work. Two candidate ideas use the same service description. “Open workbench” combines factual close-up photos, material labels and generous annotation space. “Repair journey” uses simple diagrams connecting inspection, options and approval.

The first is proposed for service storytelling because actual photographs can show workmanship. The second is proposed for explaining the process where detailed photography would obscure the sequence. They can coexist only through shared type, spacing, label treatment and approved palette roles, not by mixing unrelated mood-board pieces. The proposal does not claim customers have preferred either direction. The diagram cannot illustrate an unverified internal mechanism as product truth.

## Failure and repair

If every candidate looks like the same template, hold content constant and change the central organizing idea before changing fonts. If the result is attractive but inappropriate, identify which brief requirement it fails rather than banning its style. If product geometry drifts, return to authoritative art and repair the affected asset or composite; added texture does not repair product truth.

## Handoff and limits

Use `foundations`, `identity` or `display-art-direction` with the existing [creative direction board](../templates/creative-direction-board.md) and [visual-language specification](../templates/visual-language-system.md). Carry selected IDs, family rules and approval scope into [identity handoff](../templates/identity-handoff.md). Reuse [art direction](../draft-skills/design-foundations/references/art-direction.md) and [media quality criteria](../templates/media-quality-rubric.md).

Check visible support for the concept, equal-content comparisons, actual small/large specimens and unchanged approved assets. A coherent family does not prove distinctiveness in the market, naming clearance, product fidelity, customer preference or production readiness.
