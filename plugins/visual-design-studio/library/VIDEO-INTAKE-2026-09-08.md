# Portable video-intake exercise

This destination replaces the source project's historical worked batch with a reusable exercise. Choose an authorized public video and record its URL, retrieval date, title, creator, description, caption source and actual coverage. List outbound resources with retrieval status. Separate creator claims from inspected evidence. Route useful findings to an existing skill owner; record rejected duplicates and access gaps. Keep raw transcripts and private resources in the adopting project's permitted source store. Do not invent a completed extraction or inherit the source project's review results. Follow VIDEO-INTAKE.md for the full method.
