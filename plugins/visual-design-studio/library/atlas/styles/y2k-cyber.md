# Y2K / Cyber

## Essence

How the late 1990s and early 2000s imagined the future: translucent plastic (the iMac G3, 1998), glossy "wet" interface buttons (Mac OS X's Aqua, 2000-2001; Windows XP's Luna, 2001; Windows Vista's Aero, 2006-2007), chrome type and orbs, techno clubs, WipEout and The Designers Republic, *The Matrix* (1999) and Sega's *Jet Set Radio* (2000). Two moods share the vocabulary: Cyber (dark void, chrome, acid color, grids) and Frutiger Aero (bright sky, water, grass, bubbles), the latter named retroactively in the late 2010s. It has returned in fashion, music and nostalgia-driven consumer design since about 2020. Use it for playful, tech-flavored brands that can wear nostalgia lightly.

## Visual signature

- Gloss and translucency: pill buttons with a white top highlight, glassy panels, bubbles, droplets, light flares and lens glints.
- Chrome and liquid metal: reflective gradients on type, orbs, stars and wordmarks.
- Wide, sci-fi extended letterforms in caps (Eurostile lineage), plus a humanist sans (Frutiger/Segoe) for interface text.
- Dark void grounds with grids, HUD brackets and scanline textures; or sky-blue gradients with clouds and grass.
- Acid accents: lime, cyan, bubblegum pink against deep navy or white.
- Tech ephemera: barcodes, crosshairs, serial numbers, tiny captions, spec-sheet microtext, star-and-sparkle glyphs.

## Composition and layout

- Treat the screen like a desktop or HUD: a main "window" panel with a title bar, secondary widgets on the sides, tiny labels at the edges.
- Put the hero object (orb, chrome logo, device render) in the center with a glow and a few orbiting elements; keep everything else sparse so it reads as premium rather than cluttered.
- Pair glossy buttons with flat, quiet body panels: a clear action in Aqua with Void text, text content on solid Ice or Void panels.
- Use grids, rulers and registration marks to add technical credibility: 1px lines at 10-20% opacity.
- Dark Cyber pages: Void ground, Aqua and Bubblegum accents, a perspective grid at the floor, and sparse text. Bright Frutiger Aero pages: Ice sky gradient, clean sans, bubbles, product photography.
- Print and social: chrome type on gradients, stickers, barcodes, spec-sheet captions; keep one hero word large.

## Imagery and iconography

- 3D: chrome orbs, inflated type, bubble shapes and glossy device renders with soft reflections, rendered in real 3D (Blender, Spline) rather than flat AI blobs.
- Photography: high-flash, saturated, nature-meets-tech (water drops, grass, sky, devices); or club and cyber-fashion portraits.
- Icons: glossy filled icons with a highlight, or 1.5px outline HUD icons; both at 24-32px with strong silhouettes.
- Textures: scanlines (3-6% opacity), grain, translucent plastic; never behind text.

## Motion

Smooth and shiny. Panels open with a quick scale from 0.92 and fade (300-400ms), buttons sweep a highlight across (700ms), and hero objects float on 6-8s sine loops with small rotations. Loading states are orbiting dots or segmented bars. Use cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1) and avoid flicker or strobing; keep glow pulses slower than 1Hz. With `prefers-reduced-motion`, disable float, sheen and glow loops.

## Fits and risks

- **Where it works today:** Gen Z fashion, beauty and accessories (`audience.teens-gen-z`, `industry.fashion-apparel`, `industry.beauty-cosmetics`), music, nightlife and streaming promos (`industry.media-entertainment-streaming`, `channel.video-motion`), gaming and esports (`industry.gaming-esports`), festival microsites (`industry.events-conferences`), and social campaigns (`channel.social-media`). `archetype.jester` and `archetype.explorer` can use it for playful futurism.
- **Accessibility risk:** gloss gradients, translucency and chrome type erode contrast; small sci-fi caps and glow text hamper reading; scanlines and flicker harm photosensitive and low-vision users. Always put text on solid or high-contrast panels.
- **Longevity risk:** it rides a 25-year nostalgia cycle (peaking roughly 2020-2026) and is strongly time-stamped; the 3D chrome blob look is now an AI-generated default. Build it as a skin on stable tokens so it can be retired without redoing the system.
- **Wrong for:** `industry.healthcare-provider`, `industry.insurance`, `industry.government-civic` (credibility and calm), `tier.luxury`, `audience.older-adults` (poor legibility cues), and `audience.neurodivergent` when effects cannot be turned off.

## Do and don't

- Do commit to one mood (dark Cyber or bright Aero) and one hero object per page.
- Do render gloss with restraint: one highlight, one shadow, no more than two gradient stops per surface.
- Do keep interface text in a humanist sans on solid panels; reserve the extended face for headings and labels.
- Don't use chrome gradients on body text or put text over lens flares and bokeh.
- Don't layer glass over glass; if everything is translucent, nothing has a surface.
- Don't copy period UI literally (bevels everywhere, 1px dotted borders, animated GIF banners) unless it is the joke.

## Clichés and AI tells

- The lazy version: iridescent liquid-metal blob, "Welcome to the Future" in Orbitron, purple-to-cyan gradient, lens flares and sparkle emoji.
- Generated Y2K posters with mangled chrome lettering and random stars.
- Fix: choose a concrete reference (iMac G3 plastics, Aqua buttons, a specific club flyer), build the 3D or gloss assets properly, keep the layout structured as windows and panels, and tie the nostalgia to a real product benefit or audience memory rather than decoration.

## Accessibility notes

- On Void, Ice (17.54:1), Aqua (10.82:1), Chrome (9.81:1) and Bubblegum (7.01:1) all pass for text; the dark Cyber mode is the more accessible of the two moods.
- Cobalt #2F5BFF is 4.65:1 on Ice but 3.77:1 on Void: use it for links on light grounds only.
- Text on gloss buttons: Void on Aqua is 10.82:1; white on Aqua is 1.80:1 and fails, so do not invert.
- Sweeps and glows must not flash more than 3 times per second (WCAG 2.3.1); keep pulses slower than 1Hz and provide a reduced-effects mode.
- Translucent panels over moving backgrounds make contrast unpredictable; measure the worst case or use an opaque fallback with `@supports not (backdrop-filter: blur(1px))`.
