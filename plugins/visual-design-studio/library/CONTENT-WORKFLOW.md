## Audit and reuse

The library assigns positioning and message hierarchy to brand strategy, existing-content audit to UXD-13, and task/state wording to UXD-22. If the adopting project has valid records for those needs, reuse them. Otherwise create the selected records under those contracts; do not imply they already exist.

Optional installed helpers must be discovered and vetted on the destination. Their availability, local book paths and product-specific voices are not portable dependencies or client defaults. This library supplies the selected content guides; do not assume another host's tools exist.

## Where writing fits

| Stage | Writing decision and output | Next consumer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand | Audit existing wording; research audience language, intent, offer truth, competitors and approved voice; reference evidence IDs | Strategy and content brief |
| Establish direction | Message hierarchy, voice rules with examples, terminology, claims and CTA hierarchy; choose relevant narrative method | Content architecture, UX and visual direction |
| Explore | Page/message outlines, content model, headline directions and state-copy requirements | Wireframes, flows and compositions |
| Design | Real page copy, interface strings and channel variants with stable IDs | Layouts, components, prototypes and production |
| Validate | Facts, voice, comprehension, accessibility, localization and actual layout/state checks; actual audience tests where selected | Revisions and acceptance decision |
| Hand off | Versioned source copy, locations/keys, use limits, approved variants and refresh owners | Implementation, production, delivery and maintenance |

Writing and design iterate together. Do not finish layouts around filler and assume the final words fit. A precise authorized copy change reuses valid upstream work. Content strategy does not require regenerating brand strategy or personas for every edit.

## Methods and source boundaries

StoryBrand is a useful optional lens for persuasive brand and website messages: clarify the customer's goal and problem, the brand's supporting role, the next steps and credible outcomes/stakes. It is not a mandatory page layout or a pattern for error messages. This is our paraphrased application guidance, not a reproduction of its workbook or certification. [StoryBrand primary introduction](https://storybrand.com/downloads/your-brand-is-not-the-hero.pdf).

For task guidance, use a direct answer and a clear next action. For complex information, outline the user's questions and put the decision-critical answer first. For persuasive copy, compare a customer-story outline with a benefit/proof/action outline; select what fits intent and evidence. These are editorial heuristics, not proven conversion guarantees.

SEO writing should serve real readers and add useful, supported information rather than chase ranking signals. [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).

Error copy should explain the problem and useful recovery; separate correctable input errors from service failures. Apply guidance to the actual platform, not its visual styling. [GOV.UK error-message guidance](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-message/).

Source inspection provenance belongs to the original source version recorded in the package manifest; this adapter timestamp is not a new research date. Recheck current channel limits, platform policies and applicable accessibility standards when implementing. Keep licensed books and private source material outside the reusable package unless their inclusion is specifically permitted.

For content-only work, create or reuse the same design-work record with capability ID `content`; its name does not require a visual pack. The package catalog must retain the `content` selector; verify it resolves before selection. DISPLAY uses the campaign guide; TOUCH uses UX plus campaign for attract messages; APP/NATIVE store and launch copy uses campaign, with website guidance for a landing page.

For overlapping surfaces, choose one owner by purpose: UX owns task-triggered onboarding and transactional messages; campaign owns persuasive sequences and promotional email; editorial owns reference/help and educational articles. Other guides contribute constraints to the same unit ID rather than create duplicate copy.

## Buyer decision density

For a website content audit, tie each section to an audience question, task, useful proof or applicable requirement. Consider the decision's stakes and uncertainty before shortening it. Keep decision-critical detail available through clear navigation or progressive disclosure; separate audience routes when their tasks differ. Remove duplication and unsupported promotion without a fixed word-cut target. Preserve necessary disclosures, accessibility, search intent and the information people need to compare options. Company history or leadership earns space when it answers a relevant question.

Record the baseline, intended action and qualified outcome before judging a revision. A brief first-impression check can expose unclear purpose or next steps; also test actual task completion. Use comparable event definitions, traffic sources, devices, audiences and time windows when examining conversion changes, with sample size and uncertainty visible. A generic industry average, more clicks or an attractive screenshot cannot prove improved business performance or causation. Use the existing [content-work record](templates/content-work.md).md).
