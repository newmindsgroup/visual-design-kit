## Observed failure

The user rejected the current product/design output as fake. Inspected the fallback still of a motion fixture: an outlined SVG bottle, simple gradient, flat cap and rectangular label were placed on a photographic background. The product lacks optical glass behavior and integrated lighting. The mismatch is material, not an export-format defect. Timing, hashes, browser behavior and color fidelity do not establish photographic quality.

Inspected a separate glass product study: a more photographic generated bottle exists separately, but was never integrated into the shown motion composition. It remains an unapproved fictional asset. Previous native/export priorities must not advance rejected artwork.

## User requirement across deliverables

Product and environmental imagery must look convincingly real and professionally art-directed. No SVG/CSS product stand-ins in photographic deliverables. Vector typography, logos, diagrams and interface controls remain appropriate; do not turn these into artificial photographic objects. Stylization requires an explicit brief. Fictional imagery must not misrepresent actual client products.

## Correct production sequence

1. Identify actual versus fictional product. For actual products prioritize approved photographs, packaging artwork and dimension/material references. Hold unsupported identity details rather than invent them.
2. Establish photographic direction: camera perspective, material response, lighting, surface, contact shadows, scale, copy space and relevant references.
3. Make and inspect one hero still before adapting formats or animating. Reject cartoon geometry, plastic material, halos, inconsistent shadows, floating products, malformed labels and implausible reflections.
4. Finish typography and factual packaging precisely in editable composition. Generated label lettering is a concept until checked against approved artwork.
5. Inspect full frame and native-resolution crops, then contextual viewing size. Evaluate geometry, lighting, glass/metal/paper texture, integration, typography and hierarchy separately. A numerical average cannot override a visible defect.
6. Adapt only a visually accepted baseline. Inspect crops individually. Animate only after still quality passes; check every motion interval for identity drift, texture crawling and implausible movement.
7. Keep photographic appearance, product truth, technical export and user acceptance as separate decisions. More resolution or a different file extension cannot repair weak art direction.
