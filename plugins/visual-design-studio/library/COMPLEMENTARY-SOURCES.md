## Apply without expanding scope

In identity work, record the intended size, background, language and production medium before judging a mark. Use original concepts and exact supplied assets where locked. Separate optical alternatives requiring approval from claims of unchanged baseline preservation. Font-use rights, naming clearance, ownership and creative approval are different questions.

In physical-display work, collect the actual site, viewing distance, ambient light, approach, mounting constraints, input method, alternative access, reset/privacy behavior and maintenance conditions. Keep them pending until supplied or measured. Neither a desktop screenshot nor a standards link proves the installation works. No platform or hardware is recommended by this research.

The [reference-decision contract](REFERENCE-CONTRACT.md) records these evidence and verification needs. Its machine checks enforce declared relationships; a qualified reviewer must still assess applicability, evidence relevance and the rendered or physical result.
